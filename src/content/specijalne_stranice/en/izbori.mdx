<section>
Since state institutions have neglected their obligations for far too long, and representatives of the authorities have completely failed to meet these demands, we have been faced with massive manipulation. Those in power have on multiple occasions falsely claimed that the demands had been fulfilled, attempting to deceive citizens and suppress the righteous anger of the people’s uprising. Students therefore decided to take full responsibility for the future of this country. **We identified our tactical tool for fulfilling all the aforementioned demands in the call for snap parliamentary elections, given that the existing institutions have lost all legitimacy.** We developed a detailed and comprehensive political platform, selected experts and proven individuals to represent our movement on the electoral list, and positioned ourselves as an exceptionally powerful political actor. Our actions are now bearing visible fruit, as we achieve victories at every step and are steadily leading society toward genuine change.
</section>

# **Student Plan**

Presented here is a concise program of 11 points, divided into three sections:

The collapse of the canopy exposed the true state of our society and sparked a two-year joint struggle for justice. Now Serbia faces a clear choice: continued decline amid corruption, or a renewed state that serves every citizen, grounded in consistent respect for the Constitution and the law.

We are fighting for a National Assembly that truly reflects the people's will. We invite you to rally around the eleven steps that form the backbone of our program, organized into three sections for the country’s comprehensive recovery. With decisive action, we will halt the plunder, return stolen goods to the state budget, and invest directly in education and the economy so young people can stay here and build their futures alongside their families.

<section>
Before you are the people students have chosen to represent them in the National Assembly.

The list comprises individuals of diverse ages, professions, backgrounds, and life stories. They come from various parts of Serbia—from large cities and smaller communities alike—and from classrooms, medical practices, offices, workshops, cultural institutions, and other settings, where you see daily how the country functions when institutions do their job, and what happens when they do not.

We do not expect them to be infallible, nor do we expect them to have an answer for every question. We expect something much simpler, though, in this country, evidently much harder: that they be accountable for their actions, listen to the people they represent, explain their decisions, and understand that a parliamentary seat is not a reward, a privilege, or the start of a political career.

These are the people we have chosen to do that job.

## Get involved

On Election Day, some will be at polling tables, some will monitor what is happening outside polling stations, and others will be at call centres, behind the wheel, or in the field. We will need to transport materials, bring water and food to the people who spend hours protecting votes, and provide space, vehicles, or equipment. We will safeguard elections in many places and in many ways, which is why every role matters.

In the meantime, we are going from door to door to talk to people. That dialogue belongs to everyone. We knock on doors to listen to our neighbours, hear their concerns and questions, and invite them to join us now in returning elections to the citizens.

We don’t all have to do the same thing; what matters is that everyone contributes in their own way. Apply now to help with election monitoring, door-to-door outreach, logistics, or providing resources. We all matter in ensuring free elections.

## **The Tragedy That Sparked It All**

On November 1, 2024, a concrete canopy at the newly renovated railway station in Novi Sad collapsed, killing 16 people and injuring others.

The lack of institutional response triggered blockades of university faculties and high schools, daily traffic blockades accompanied by 15—and later 16—minutes of silence, and mass protests across the country, which began nearly two years ago and continue to this day.

The largest protest took place on March 15, 2025, at Slavija Square in Belgrade.

According to the Archive of Public Gatherings, between 275,000 and 325,000 people gathered at Slavija that night, making it one of the largest protests in Serbia's modern history.

This protest drew global attention, while the police and the country’s leadership faced backlash over the alleged use of a [sonic weapon](https://studenti.org.rs/afere/) against protesters.

The authorities continue to deny the use of the weapon, despite thousands of reports, victim testimonies, and video recordings.

## **The Final Step Toward Lasting Changes**

![](@assets/images/94da10d2-62d2-4fd0-a253-6fe0fd5b07df_rw_1920.webp)

Smear campaigns targeted most protests and marches, and physical attacks targeted students and citizens.

This only spurred even more people to take to the streets. In response to persistent failure to accept accountability and rising social tensions, students formed **the Student List**, a carefully curated electoral list of independent candidates from across Serbia who will run in the snap parliamentary elections on **October 25, 2026.**

The only way to bring about significant, lasting change in a failing, corruption-riddled system is to replace it with a new one—led by honest, capable individuals who will drive that change forward.
</section>

## **Victory Calls** 

**The March From October 16 to October 21**

The marches toward Serbia's major cities began as a form of protest and an expression of solidarity with the families of the victims. One group of students even cycled to Strasbourg to voice the Serbian people's grievances before European institutions.

These marches are now the beating heart of the movement. Every kilometer covered is a step toward justice and freedom, and a reminder that accountability and perseverance matter. On October 16, students will set out from every major city in Serbia and converge on Slavija Square.

They will arrive on October 21—the day before the final rally, titled “Victory Calls”—bringing the movement to a pinnacle right before the election silence begins.
</section>

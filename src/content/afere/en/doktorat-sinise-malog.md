title: Siniša Mali's PhD Dissertation
---

In 2014, on the Peščanik website, Raša Karapandža challenged the validity of Siniša Mali's PhD dissertation. Its content was found to have been taken from various sources without those sources being cited in the dissertation. Mali "defended" his PhD at the Faculty of Organizational Sciences. Mali was never awarded a PhD degree. Eleven years have passed since then, and the case has still not been resolved in court.

**How did Siniša Mali obtain a PhD despite all the irregularities in his dissertation?**

title: Respirators
---

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Serbian authorities procured much-needed respirators to keep hospitalized patients alive in a very non-transparent manner. Without public tenders and additional details about the procurement of the respirators, the authorities procured these instruments at questionable prices and of questionable quality, and often without public information about where they were purchased and where many of these respirators ended up.

Despite the public reaction and the filing of criminal charges against corruption and misuse of public funds, the competent institutions never investigated the case of the disputed respirators, their origin, and quality. During critical times, we witnessed the ruling party's deception and lack of transparency. This was one of the many steps leading to distrust in state institutions and competent authorities.

**When will we have the right to the truth?**

title: Collapse of the Canopy at the Novi Sad Railway Station
---

On 1 November 2024, the canopy of Wing B at the Novi Sad Railway Station collapsed. It took 16 lives. Following public pressure, only a part of the project documentation, which remains incomplete even six months later, was published. The project is riddled with inconsistencies, such as the fact that work on the station building was not reported until October 16, 2024, three months after its official opening. Whether it was human negligence or a mistake, someone must be held accountable.

title: Man in a Barrel – Murder at the 27 Restaurant
---

Aleksandar Nešović was murdered on May 12, 2026, **at the 27 Restaurant in Senjak**, and his remains were found **nine days later in a buried barrel near Inđija**. On July 10, the Higher Prosecutor’s Office filed an indictment against Saša Vuković for murder, while five other people were charged on **suspicion of destroying evidence and helping cover up the crime**.

At the center of the affair is the then **Chief of the Belgrade Police, Veselin Milić**. According to the indictment, Milić was a **family friend** of the accused Vuković; he knew about Vuković’s conflict with Nešović, and participated in arranging their meeting. The prosecution claims that the owner of the restaurant informed him that Nešović had been killed, but **the Chief of the Police did not report it**. The following day, he allegedly **entered false information into the official note** and omitted information about his connections with the actors in the case.

The prosecution changed the description of Milić’s role during the investigation: he was initially suspected of aiding the perpetrator. That part was later dropped, but he was charged with failing to report the murder and forging an official document.

**How is it possible for the top man in the Belgrade police to learn about a murder and not report it?** Who kept Milić at the top of the system for years in which the boundaries between the police, private connections, and crime have almost completely disappeared?

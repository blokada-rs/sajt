title: Doljevac Toll Plaza
---

The traffic accident at the Doljevac toll plaza occurred on January 31, 2019, when a vehicle belonging to Koridori Srbije struck a car at the toll plaza, killing Stanika Gligorijević and injuring several people, including the then director Zoran Babić. The driver, Dejan Stanojević, was sentenced to four and a half years' imprisonment. Controversy was sparked by the disappearance of two minutes of surveillance camera footage, which, according to the family and lawyers, could reveal all the circumstances of the accident and who was behind the wheel that night.

In August 2019, Vučić stated that he had watched the entire footage of the accident, called it "horrible", claimed that Babić had not been behind the wheel and promised to request that the footage be made publicly available. In April 2021, he stated that he did not have the recording, that no one had requested it and that he had not provided it because no one had asked him for it.

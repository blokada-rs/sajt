title: Murder of Oliver Ivanović
---

Oliver Ivanović, one of the few politicians in Kosovo who criticized both Belgrade and the criminal structures of the Serbian List, was killed on January 16, 2018, from behind, with six shots to the back and legs, in front of his office in North Mitrovica.

For months before that, he had been the target of a media campaign on TV Pink and in pro-regime tabloids. 

**The main suspects are connected to the security structures and crime, but have never been prosecuted.**

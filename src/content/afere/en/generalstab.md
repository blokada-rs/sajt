Just two weeks after the tragedy at the Novi Sad Railway Station, using the public’s state of collective shock as a smokescreen, top officials from the executive branch, acting in secret and bypassing both official experts and the expert community, pushed through unconstitutional and unlawful decisions at a Government session. These decisions revoked the status of "immovable cultural property" from the General Staff and Ministry of Defence complex in Belgrade.

Despite relevant heritage conservation expertise stressing that these structures have not lost their monumental value, the controversial decisions were nonetheless made without any expert analysis, without proposals from the competent institutions, and without any legal basis. All of this was done to pave the way for the construction of the first Trump hotel in Europe, through an arrangement with Jared Kushner and an agreement reached with absolutely no transparency toward the public.

The filing of charges against Nikola Selaković marks the first time in the history of our country that a sitting minister has been indicted. This demonstrates that, despite systemic corruption, our institutions are capable of holding politicians accountable, thanks to the efforts and courage of many individuals.

title: Burning of Milan Jovanović’s House
---

The house of journalist Milan Jovanović was set on fire on the night of December 11, 2018, in retaliation for his investigative reporting on abuses in the Municipality of Grocka. A Molotov cocktail was thrown into the garage while Jovanović and his wife, Jela, were sleeping. The flames quickly spread and engulfed their home, but they did not swallow the truth.

This is not just an attack on one man, but an attempt to intimidate and silence all who dare to stand up to corruption.

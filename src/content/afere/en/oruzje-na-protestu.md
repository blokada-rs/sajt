title: Weapon at the Protest
---

At the “15 for 15” protest, the largest in Serbia’s history, held on March 15, 2025, in Belgrade, a fifteen-minute silence began precisely at 7 PM to honor the victims of the canopy collapse. Hundreds of thousands of people stood quietly in the city center, at Slavija Square, along Kralja Milana Street, and around the National Assembly.

While the tribute was underway, attacks broke out near the Assembly against student marshals and citizens standing in silence. Bottles, stones, and pyrotechnics were hurled at the crowd from the direction of Pionirski Park and a nearby construction site, while marshals attempted to prevent clashes and protect the citizens.

In the eleventh minute of the tribute, precisely at 7:11 PM, a sudden, unexplained sound swept through Kralja Milana Street, accompanied by a shockwave that caused the crowd to scatter toward both sides of the street almost simultaneously. Civil society organizations collected over 3.000 testimonies from people describing the same moment and the physical distress that followed.

The authorities immediately dismissed the possibility that a sonic weapon had been used, even before any independent investigation could take place. Initially, they claimed the police did not possess such devices; however, they later admitted that the Ministry of Internal Affairs had acquired LRADs in 2021 and that one was present near the National Assembly during the protest. Footage analysis and testimonies collected by the organization “Earshot” concluded that a targeted attack using a directed acoustic device had very likely occurred. The European Court of Human Rights ordered Serbia to prevent the use of such devices against assembled citizens. At the same time, the Council of Europe Commissioner warned in May 2026 that despite thousands of submitted testimonies, no official investigation had been launched.

The Higher Public Prosecutor’s Office subsequently focused its suspicions on us and on citizens who had spoken publicly about the event. It based its narrative of an alleged “simulation” on the minutes of a security meeting we held two months earlier, when the March 15 protest had not yet been planned. At that meeting, we had discussed sound and water cannons, tear gas, and other means of coercion as potential risks to participants in peaceful protests. Today, the Prosecutor’s Office is using responsible preparation for potential repression to blame those who may have been subjected to that very repression.

For over a year, institutions have failed to determine the cause of the mass panic. The regime is now trying to turn witnesses into suspects and close a matter it has failed to clarify. Thousands of eyewitness accounts and video recordings of the event will not disappear because of the Prosecutor’s Office statement.

**Who caused the fear and panic on March 15, 2025, at 7:11 PM along Kralja Milana Street, during a peaceful protest and a 15-minute silence in memory of those who tragically lost their lives in Novi Sad?**

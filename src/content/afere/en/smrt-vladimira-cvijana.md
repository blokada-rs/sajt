title: Death of Vladimir Cvijan
---

Vladimir Cvijan, **former Secretary General of the Presidency of Serbia**, MP, and **a senior official of the SNS**, was one of the first people from the party leadership to oppose Aleksandar Vučić openly. In late 2013, he announced his candidacy for the presidency of the SNS and accused Vučić of turning sessions of the party presidency into compiling lists for the arrest of political opponents. **After a conflict within the party leadership, he also filed criminal charges for an alleged attempt at physical assault, left the SNS, and then withdrew from public life.**

**His body was found on January 5, 2018, in the Danube.** The Higher Prosecutor’s Office concluded that he died as a result of drowning, that there were no injuries on the body that would indicate a criminal offense, and the case was archived. The public, however, did not even know that Cvijan was dead for more than three years. **The information reached the media only in March 2021**, when a document on the costs of the autopsy was discovered. His urn has since been placed in the Lešće cemetery, under a plaque that simply says “Vladimir”.

The institutions closed the case before the public learned that it existed.

**Who is responsible for the three years of silence, and how could the death of a former state and party official go without a single public response?**

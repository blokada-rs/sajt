title: Intensive Care Unit
---

On March 17, 2025, Serbian President Aleksandar Vučić, accompanied by journalists, entered the ICU at the University Clinical Center of Serbia (UCCS). His unauthorized visit to the injured in the nightclub fire in Kočani, North Macedonia, only endangered their health.  Individuals were prohibited from entering the ICU, and on that day, a ban on visiting UCCS was in effect due to the ongoing flu epidemic.

**We demand accountability for this incident, and we ask, who is to blame?**

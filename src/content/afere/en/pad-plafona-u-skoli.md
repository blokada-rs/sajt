title: Ceiling Collapse Incident at Elementary School
---

On February 14, 2025, during a class at Slobodan Bajić Paja Elementary School in Pećinci, a piece of plaster fell from the wall onto two sixth-grade students. The girls did not sustain serious injuries. This incident marks the third occurrence of injuries resulting from a ceiling collapse at the school. Despite repeated incidents, no official response has been issued.

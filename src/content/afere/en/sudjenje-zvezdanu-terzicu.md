title: Trial of Zvezdan Terzić
---

Zvezdan Terzić — accused of strong-arming players and embezzling 1.1 million German marks. He also enabled foreign companies, whose ownership has not been determined, to earn 3.3 million.

The trial began in January 2011, and Terzić had meanwhile become the director of the Red Star soccer club. He did this, as he himself said, at the insistence of Aleksandar Vučić, then First Deputy Prime Minister.

The trial was postponed due to the defendants’ absences, and it was also restarted several times due to the repeated changes of judges.

Numerous delays played into Terzić’s hands, as two of the three charges against him had expired, as Judge Ana Trifunović Đuričković noted when she explained the acquittal in July last year.

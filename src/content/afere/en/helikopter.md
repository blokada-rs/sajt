title: Helicopter
---

A Serbian Army helicopter departed on March 13, 2015, following standard procedure. However, it was subsequently instructed to delay landing to allow media coverage of the baby’s arrival from Novi Pazar.

Despite adverse weather conditions and explicit warnings, the helicopter was redirected to a civilian airport. This decision prompted several questions, including why it did not land at the Military Medical Academy or Batajnica Air Base, and why it was flown to Belgrade instead of a hospital closer to the accident site.

**There were no answers.**

The reports were marked as “top secret”. The Minister of Defense continued his career. The Higher Public Prosecutor’s Office decided that “no one is guilty”.

**And seven people, including the baby and two doctors, never returned home.**

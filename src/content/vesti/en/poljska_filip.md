## Lunch with Prince Philip

**Students and Student List candidates** will attend a lunch with Prince Philip on Thursday, **19 November**.

## Polish Independence Day

The Embassy of the Republic of Poland in Belgrade has invited representatives of **the Student List and the Student Movement** to attend a ceremony marking the Independence Day of the Republic of Poland and the Day of the Armed Forces of the Republic of Poland.

The ceremony will be held on **19 November at the Radisson Collection Hotel, Old Mill Belgrade**, attended by members of the diplomatic corps, representatives of Serbia's future institutions, and figures from public, scientific, and cultural life.

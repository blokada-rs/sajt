People of Serbia,

At our last rally before these parliamentary elections, we told you that they were a referendum between Serbia and the mafia, between freedom and slavery, between prosperity and decline.

Serbia has made its choice clearly. It has shown everyone who ever doubted it that we were right to believe in it and that it is worth fighting for. Every tyrant, past or future, now knows more surely than ever that Serbia cannot be silenced.

We have removed the heaviest shackles from a wounded but proud Serbia, though not the only ones. No election victory blinds us to the fact that the students' demands have still not been met and that our people still do not live in dignity. We owe it to all of you to keep going until what we rose up for becomes reality, and until, with our combined strength and effort, we carry out the programme of the Student List.

Last night, all across Serbia, we sang, danced the kolo and celebrated. We celebrated what we have achieved, and now it is time again for work and sweat. There is still a lot of work ahead of us.

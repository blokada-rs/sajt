## Problems in applying the law

The Law on Caregivers and Parent Caregivers, which is at its core discriminatory towards most persons with disabilities who need care, as well as towards most carers, has already shown all its shortcomings in its first days of application. It has also revealed the outgoing government's open tendency to narrow down, as far as possible, the circle of people who will be granted carer status.

To all the citizens who have contacted us in recent days, and who are rightly disappointed and angry at the irresponsible work of the competent ministry, we say that **resolving their status will be one of the priorities in the field of social protection** as soon as we take over the levers of executive power.

## A thorough reform of the social protection system

To the staff of social protection institutions, we say that a thorough reform of the system lies ahead. Everyone who does their job responsibly will be empowered, while those who have been holding the system back will be held accountable.

**It must never again happen that the most vulnerable pay the price for an irresponsible social policy.**

## Quality care without discrimination

In line with its Programme, and with the broadest possible participation of persons with disabilities and their family members, the Student List will take all necessary legislative, institutional, budgetary, and other measures.

The goal is for **quality care services to be available to all persons with disabilities who need them**, and for carers' employment status to be resolved in a dignified way and without discrimination.

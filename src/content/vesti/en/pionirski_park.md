The Students in Blockade will hold a press conference on Thursday, 29 October, at 12 noon, in the liberated Pionirski Park in Belgrade.

This will be our first press conference since election day, 25 October. We will set out concrete goals and talk about the next steps of the student movement.

After a historic election day, we will keep communicating clearly and openly with the public about everything that comes next.

We invite members of the press to attend the conference.

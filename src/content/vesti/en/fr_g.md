Representatives of the **Student List** are continuing talks with representatives of international institutions and diplomatic delegations. Meetings with representatives of France, Germany, and the Council of Europe are planned in the coming period, covering European integration, Serbia's future, the rule of law, and cooperation with European partners.

### Meetings with the French delegation

On the French side, the meetings will be attended by **the Minister Delegate for Europe and the Ambassador of France to Serbia**, together with representatives of the French Parliament, the Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs, and experts.

**The Student List will be represented by our candidates:**

- Dejan Šoškić
- Jasmina Đorđević
- Vladan Jovanović

<br />


### Meeting with a member of the German Bundestag

On Wednesday, 18 November, from 15:45 to 16:45, there will be a meeting with **Boris Mijatović, a member of the German Bundestag for the Greens**.

The talks will cover the current situation in Serbia, plans for forming a new government, and Serbia's relations with Germany and the European Union.

**The Student List will be represented by candidate Jasmina Đorđević.**

### Talks with the Council of Europe

Also on Wednesday, 18 November, at 16:00, a meeting with representatives of **the Council of Europe** is planned.

The talks will focus on opportunities for cooperation and support in the areas of human rights, the judiciary, and the rule of law. Particular topics will include the fight against corruption, judicial and constitutional reform, the electoral process, the media, citizens' democratic participation, inclusion, and public trust in institutions.

**The Student List will be represented by candidate Vladimir Jovanović.**

## Open to dialogue, accountable to citizens

Meetings with international partners are an opportunity to talk directly about the situation in Serbia, present our views, and share experiences. We believe it is important to build relations based on openness, mutual respect, and cooperation.

In these talks, we start from the values and principles we stand for, and from the trust citizens have placed in us. **We want partnerships that contribute to Serbia's development, while preserving our independence in making decisions about its future.**

We thank all our interlocutors for their interest and willingness to engage in dialogue. We will continue to keep the public informed in good time about our meetings and talks.

**The students prevailed!**

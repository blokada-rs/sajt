At the invitation to celebrate Algeria's National Day, held on 16 November in Belgrade at the **Hyatt Regency** hotel, representatives of the Student List – Students Win will attend, together with a large group of students.

The invitation highlighted the historical ties of friendship and cooperation between Algeria and Serbia, and extended warm congratulations on our list's victory in the early parliamentary elections of 25 October 2026.

The Student List – Students Win will be represented by:

- **Sara El Sarag**
- **Zoran Stanić**
- **Dejan Radivojević**
- **Vladan Jovanović**
- **Bojan Dimitrijević**

They will be joined by a large group of students, as we want to help foster friendly relations and build connections between young people from Serbia and Algeria.

This invitation is an opportunity to keep fostering friendly relations and to develop cooperation between the two countries. We believe that international relations are built through open dialogue and mutual respect, preserving historical ties while opening up space for new forms of cooperation.

**The students prevailed!**

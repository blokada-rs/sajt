We understand the joy, the emotions and the need to finally celebrate the victory after everything we have been through. But now it is important that we stay dignified and responsible.

We do not support breaking into media outlets and institutions, and we do not call for it. We will free the media in the National Assembly, through an institutional fight for freedom and professional journalism.

We ask all citizens to disperse peacefully and head home tonight once the celebration on Slavija is over.

We won this victory together. Let's keep it dignified!

Instead of celebrating freedom and victory on Sunday evening, students and citizens of Ub who had gathered peacefully on the Town Square to celebrate the victory were attacked.

According to the testimony of those present, at around 1:30 after midnight a large group of supporters of the former regime, armed with sticks, clubs and firearms, set upon the people there.

Two people were injured in the attack. One of them suffered injuries to the nose and knee after being hit with a club.

When an eyewitness went to help the injured man, members of the Ub citizens' assembly (zbor) also arrived to help. A few minutes later, however, the same group attacked the gathering again, this time also targeting members of the assembly. One of them suffered serious bodily injuries.

Both injured people were treated at the General Hospital in Valjevo, which they reached by their own means.

**Today it will be decided whether these people's detention is extended by 30 days or whether they are released.**

We appeal to our dear fellow citizens not to go out to gatherings if they are released, so that we avoid new clashes and keep everyone safe.

The Student List candidates have not yet entered the National Assembly or taken their oath as members of parliament.

**When that moment comes, the change of government begins, and everyone who must answer before the law will be held accountable.**

For the past two years we have been dignified and patient. Let's stay that way just a little longer. **We are not giving up on justice, but we will not let ourselves be drawn into new clashes either.**

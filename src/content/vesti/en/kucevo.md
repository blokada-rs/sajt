Lately, certain individuals have been going from house to house, presenting themselves as organisers of the student uprising and speaking on behalf of the Student Movement in Kučevo without the approval of the Student Movement itself.

The Student Movement in Kučevo came about thanks to students and citizens who came together around a shared struggle for justice, freedom, and accountability. Its work has been built through **shared effort, trust, and solidarity** among people who were ready from the start to take responsibility for their community.

Our struggle did not begin as a political project or as an effort to win office. It was set in motion by demands for justice, accountability, and changes that would ensure institutions work in the interest of citizens. Only later, through collective decision-making, were elections recognised as a democratic means of fulfilling the students' demands. **The decision to take part in elections is not a licence for political horse-trading, for unilateral decision-making, or for individuals to appropriate the results of a shared struggle.**

From the very beginning, there have been different opinions in Kučevo about how to organise and direct the protests. Nevertheless, **positions and decisions concerning the movement must be the result of collective agreement**, not of the personal ambitions or political interests of individuals.

## Attempts to speak on behalf of the movement

Lately, certain individuals have been going from house to house, presenting themselves as organisers of the student uprising and speaking on behalf of the Student Movement in Kučevo. At the same time, they are announcing candidates for the local elections and political arrangements, even though the Student Movement has made no official decision on these matters. Such conduct misleads citizens and tries to create the impression that these individuals have powers that neither the community nor the movement has entrusted to them.

That is why we say clearly: **no one can declare themselves a representative of the Student Movement, appropriate its work, or make political decisions on behalf of everyone.** Trust is earned through work and responsibility towards the community, and roles and powers are determined through agreed democratic procedures. This applies to everyone, regardless of their political views, past involvement, or personal ambitions.

## Support for the municipal coordinator

We particularly emphasise that **our municipal coordinator, M. M., has the support of the student movement and the community and will continue to perform the role entrusted to him.** His work and contribution to building the movement in Kučevo cannot be undone by public call-outs, unfounded accusations, and attempts to discredit him. All disagreements should be resolved within the community, through dialogue and agreement, not through pressure and campaigns aimed at individuals.

## On the events of 25 October

Regarding the events of 25 October, during election night, accusations were made publicly against our coordinator. According to the statement of our colleague and witness, there was a verbal argument on that occasion, but no physical confrontation. **We condemn every form of violence** and believe that such events must be established on the basis of verifiable facts, not one-sided posts and public call-outs. Unverified accusations must not become a tool for political discrediting.

## Who decides about the local elections

We remind everyone that **the local elections in Kučevo have not yet been called.** No decisions have been made about a possible electoral list, candidates, or a candidate for mayor. All future political steps will be decided democratically, in line with the principles and procedures of the Student Movement. **No one has the right to announce those decisions in advance on our behalf.**

We call on local politicians to act their age, refrain from public call-outs and unfounded accusations, stick to verifiable facts, and show that they can offer citizens more than hatred and division. Kučevo needs **dialogue, accountability, and concrete solutions**, and political action should be measured by what is done for the community, not by attacks on those who think differently.

**The Student Movement in Kučevo is no one's political refuge, shortcut to power, or vehicle for personal ambition.** We will not allow anyone to appropriate citizens' trust and the results of our shared struggle.

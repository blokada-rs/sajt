**The constitutive session of the National Assembly of the Republic of Serbia has been scheduled for 17 November at 10:00.**

The proposed agenda includes the confirmation of MPs’ mandates and the election of the Speaker and Deputy Speakers of the National Assembly, the Secretary General, the working bodies, and the members of the standing delegations to international institutions.

Until the Speaker is elected, the session will be chaired by the **eldest MP**.

Once the National Assembly is constituted, the **90-day deadline for electing the Government of the Republic of Serbia** begins to run.

**A new era for Serbia is coming!**

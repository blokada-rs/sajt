A strange story has been doing the rounds in Vranje. Folks are sayin’ the students have already struck deals, already shared out the posts, and that everybody already knows who’s gonna sit in the mayor’s chair. Funny thing is, seems everybody found out before the students did. So the town doesn’t have to keep guessin’, here’s how things stand.

Dear citizens,

The student movement was born out of the struggle for justice, freedom, and a state whose institutions serve citizens, not the politically powerful. We are not a party, nor are we anyone’s extended arm.

We won the parliamentary elections, and that mandate belongs neither to us nor to anyone who would like to wear it as a badge. It belongs to all citizens of Serbia, from Vranje to Subotica.

The constitutive session of the National Assembly is the start of an obligation we owe to all of you, not a chance for local strongmen to push their own people into positions.

### The local elections have not even been called

That is why we will not stay silent while certain individuals already present themselves as if they had the student movement’s backing for the local elections. Still less will we allow political negotiations to be held, deals to be struck, and posts to be shared out in advance behind the student movement’s name.

### One thing must be unambiguous

The student movement and the Student List candidates have no deals, alliances, or staffing arrangements with:

- **Slobodan Petrović,**
- **Igor Andonov,**
- **Isidora Stefanović,**
- **Dejan Stanojević Denzi,**
- **Igor Zlatković,**
- **Professor Dragan Antić, and**
- **Perica Janković.**

**They do not speak on behalf of the student movement, they do not work with Student List candidates**, and they do not have our support for any public office, including the office of mayor or a candidacy in the upcoming local elections.

Any claim that there are agreements on cooperation, on dividing up posts, or on supporting particular candidates is untrue. No one has been given the right to negotiate on behalf of the student movement, to promise posts, or to dispose of the trust the citizens of Serbia have placed in us.

### Support is not a contract

Support that someone gave during the election process is not a political contract. It creates no right to a favour in return, a post, or influence over decisions that belong to the citizens. An election victory is not spoils to be divided among political players.

While we wait for the National Assembly to be constituted, and while in Vranje names are being put forward for top seats most loudly of all, we want to say it clearly and in advance: **posts are not decided behind closed doors.**

### Pressure will not throw us off course

We warn in particular that we will not tolerate **pressure and threats** directed at members of the municipal team, students, and their associates. Attempts at intimidation will not shake our determination, nor will they secure anyone a political position.

Our obligation is to all citizens of Serbia, not to individuals who see the victory as a chance for personal gain. Public office is not a **reward**, a means of **political survival**, or a right anyone c**an grant themselves.**

**The students won, so:**

- **No trading in citizens’ trust.**
- **No dividing up posts behind closed doors.**
- **No one is above the law!**

Recently, through social media, **we have been receiving a growing number of messages from concerned citizens and construction sector employees **, who point to serious problems in the operations of certain private construction companies. According to the employees, workers are losing their jobs and some firms are suspending or significantly scaling back their activities, while at the same time serious questions are being raised about how contracts financed with the money of the citizens of the Republic of Serbia are awarded and carried out.

This raises a simple question: **why is the Government of the Republic of Serbia, which is serving its term as the outgoing government, not fulfilling its obligations and not taking measures to protect the public interest and the rights of employees?**

We stress in particular that this is the outgoing government, which still bears full institutional and political responsibility for the decisions it makes and the actions it takes. **The future government has no authority whatsoever over the work of the current Government, its decisions, its public procurement procedures, or the operations of companies that win state contracts.** It is therefore unacceptable for responsibility for current events to be shifted onto those who do not run the institutions and have no way of influencing their decisions.

How is it possible that companies that have operated for years as privileged contractors, amid numerous controversies surrounding their business and the individuals linked to them, continue to win contracts funded with public money, while at the same time there are allegations of contractual obligations not being met, works being halted, and employees' livelihoods being put at risk?

Of particular concern are allegations of non-transparent public procurement procedures, possible irregularities in the awarding of contracts, and a lack of proper oversight of contract implementation. If these allegations are well founded, responsibility cannot be shifted onto workers, citizens, or the future government. **Every public dinar must leave a clear trail, and every contractual obligation must come with accountability.**

Why are political scores being settled on the backs of citizens and employees?

We understand that we are in a period of political and institutional transition. We also understand that there are different political interests and conflicts. However, what we cannot and will not accept is that citizens' living standards, their jobs, and their livelihoods become a means of political pressure or the subject of mutual score-settling.

**The interests of citizens and the dignity of workers must stand above any political interest!**

**We call on the competent institutions** to investigate without delay all allegations of irregularities **in the construction sector**, including:

1. public procurement procedures and the criteria for awarding contracts;
2. the fulfilment of contractual obligations and the actual situation on construction sites;
3. flows of public money and the justification of payments made;
4. respect for labour rights, payment of wages, and the protection of employees;
5. any abuse of office and misuse of budget funds.

We remind all competent authorities, institutions, businesses, and individuals that no one is, or may be, **above the law**. **We will closely monitor developments in the construction sector**, from the awarding of contracts, through the execution of contracted works, to respect for workers' rights and safety on construction sites.

We will not allow the burden of irresponsibility, possible abuses, and political score-settling to fall on the shoulders of people whose work provides for their families.

Do not make citizens' lives even harder during the transition period.

**Settle your political scores in the political arena. Manage public money responsibly. Apply the law equally to everyone. And let workers work and earn an honest living.**

**Jobs are not a political weapon. People's livelihoods are not a tool for blackmail. Public money is no one's private property.**

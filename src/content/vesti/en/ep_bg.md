On Saturday, 14 November, **students and Student List candidate Mario Reljanović will meet a Member of the European Parliament from The Left group who is also a member of Italy's Five Star Movement.** The meeting has been organised at the invitation of the Center for the Politics of Emancipation.

The visit to Belgrade has been organised so that the MEP can learn more about the current political and socio-economic situation in Serbia, particularly in the context of the European integration process. Talks with representatives of trade unions, the media, and the academic community are planned during the visit.

**We believe it is important to discuss the situation in Serbia with representatives of the international community and to share our experiences, views, and vision for the country's future with them directly.** We are open to talking with anyone interested in hearing our views and exchanging opinions with us.

We see international cooperation as an opportunity to share experiences, build partnerships, and foster a better understanding of the situation in Serbia. **In these talks, we start from our own positions and the needs of citizens, whose voice and trust are the foundation of everything we do.**

We thank the Center for the Politics of Emancipation for the invitation, and everyone who has shown interest in talking with the students. We will continue to keep the public informed in good time about our meetings and activities.

**The students prevailed!**

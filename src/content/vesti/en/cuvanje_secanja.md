**In memory of our colleague Vuk, the Vuk Matić Foundation has been established**, so that his kindness, creativity, and love for the world can live on through work that leaves a lasting mark.

The Foundation has two goals: **planting at least 8,160 trees**, one for every day Vuk spent with us, and organising annual LEGO challenge workshops and competitions to encourage curiosity and creativity in new generations.

**We invite all of you to be part of this story**, and to support the Foundation's work by donating, planting trees, suggesting locations, or volunteering.

**Let's make sure together that Vuk's spirit lives on through what we create and leave behind.**

You can find more information about the Foundation and ways to support it at [vukfondacija.com](https://vukfondacija.com)

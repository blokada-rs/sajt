Dear fellow fighters,

**These past few days we have celebrated the victory we waited for so long, but now we would like to invite all of you to a volunteer work day!**

As you know, over these two years our city has gone through changes that are not exactly pleasant to the eyes. That is why we have decided to ask you to help us make our buildings, streets and parks shine again! We ask all citizens who are able to give even a little of their time and energy to community work, whether in your own neighbourhood or somewhere else in the city.

Bring garden tools, rubbish bags and seedlings, roll up your sleeves and put on your gloves. And if nighttime  catches up with you, remember to bring your flashlights. The messages and symbols of hatred that have been defacing our walls for a long time are no exception, so we ask all you artistic souls to bring your gear and join us in renewing our surroundings.

**We will build and beautify our city. It is time to give it back its spark and shine!**

This call has no time limit: the initiative starts today and will last until the job is done!

With love,

Your students!

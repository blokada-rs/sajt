What happened to ALTA Bank's records? 

Has its electronic data been preserved? 

What did the National Bank of Serbia find during its inspections, and what measures have the competent institutions taken?

These are questions to which citizens have the right to get answers.

The National Bank of Serbia revoked ALTA Bank's operating licence on 31 October, and bankruptcy proceedings were opened on 3 November 2026. At a time when assets, equipment, and archives are being taken over and staff are leaving, there is a risk that important records and electronic data will be lost. That is why it must be established right away what has been preserved, where it is, and who is responsible for it.

We invite you to fill in and submit, on our website, **three requests for access to information of public importance**: to the Deposit Insurance Agency, the National Bank of Serbia, and the Administration for the Prevention of Money Laundering.

We are asking for information on the safekeeping of records and electronic systems, the inspections carried out, the irregularities identified, and how the competent institutions acted. We are not asking for data on individual clients or transactions. We are asking the institutions to explain what they have done and to take responsibility for their actions.

The election victory did not mean that all the levers of power had already changed hands. The SNS government is still in a caretaker capacity, and presidential elections lie ahead. But accountability cannot wait for every institution to change. The records must be preserved now.

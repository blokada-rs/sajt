On election day, 25 October 2026, members of a Student List mobile team were attacked in Niš while touring polling stations. Near the Fire Station, a group of people used cars to block our members' way. According to their testimony, the attackers were armed with metal bars, and one member of the team was physically assaulted.

Six people close to the Serbian Progressive Party have been arrested on suspicion of taking part in the attack. Among them is Branislav Kačar, a former city councillor for sport and adviser to minister Novica Tončev. During a search of his flat, narcotics were found, as well as expensive watches and cigarettes.

This attack was not an isolated case. During election day, citizens reported that a group of around ten cars was also appearing at other locations in the city. **It was an organised attempt to intimidate those who were safeguarding the citizens' electoral will.**

That attempt failed. The citizens of Niš voted and chose change. **The time when violence and intimidation went unpunished is behind us.**

**From now on, the law will apply equally to everyone, without exceptions and without political protection**. Everyone who took part in the attacks, as well as those who organised them, will answer for it in accordance with the law. Without revenge, but also without a cover-up.

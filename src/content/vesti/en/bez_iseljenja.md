## We call on public bailiffs to refrain from the announced evictions

We call on public bailiffs to refrain from carrying out the forced evictions of families in Zemun and Vračar announced for 2 and 4 November.

**Until effective legal protection is ensured, no one should be exposed to the irreversible consequences of losing their home.** Public authority entails responsibility towards citizens, not merely carrying out a procedure. **The right to a home and human dignity must take precedence over private profit.**

Public bailiffs hold public authority and carry out their work independently. Their income is tied to debt collection and the costs of proceedings, which creates a risk of conflict between the public interest and private gain. **Citizens must not lose their homes because of a system in which costs keep rising while effective protection of their rights is lacking.**

Small debts can grow many times over because of enforcement costs. Properties are sold at prices well below market value, so citizens can end up both without a home and with remaining debt. Starting court proceedings often does not stop a sale or an eviction, even when the lawfulness of the enforcement is being challenged. **Irreversible consequences must not occur before citizens have been given effective judicial protection.**

**A home is a right, not a privilege.**

**The Student List's programme provides for the protection of a household's sole home that meets its basic housing needs.** That is why we will amend the Law on Enforcement and Security, extend the protection of the right to a home, ensure judicial oversight and prevent abuse.

We will build an enforcement system based on humanity, fairness and accountability: a system that allows debts to be collected, but does not leave citizens without basic legal protection.

**The state must protect its citizens, not make it possible to profit from their misfortune.**

**Studentska lista – Studenti pobeđuju (Student List – Students Are Winning)**

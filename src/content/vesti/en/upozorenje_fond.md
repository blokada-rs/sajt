**The Student Movement warns employees of the Republic Health Insurance Fund** (RFZO), and those responsible in the institutions that manage public property, that any unauthorised destruction, removal, alteration or concealment of official documents, whether on paper or in electronic form, is unacceptable and carries liability under the law, including criminal liability.

We call on all employees to preserve complete documentation, electronic databases, official records, and correspondence. No one has the right to carry out unlawful orders or to destroy the traces of their own actions. A change of management does not erase responsibility.

At the same time, we demand an urgent explanation of the circumstances in which two notices were published on 28 October 2026 for the long-term lease of commercial premises in Smederevo, owned by the Smederevo Health Centre and the City of Smederevo.

These are premises where a pharmacy business already operates, and the terms of the notices require bidders to have at least 300 pharmacies, 700 employed pharmacists, annual revenue of 50 billion dinars and at least 25 years of operation in the pharmacy sector.

The question arises as to why such high requirements have been set for leasing premises that house only three pharmacies, and whether their real effect is to restrict competition and prevent the existing Smederevo Pharmacy Institution from continuing to operate.

Of particular concern are the short deadline for submitting bids and the fact that interested bidders do not have access to basic data on the number and structure of employees, on pharmacy revenues, or other information needed to prepare a bid.

We demand that the competent institutions answer publicly who set these conditions, on the basis of which criteria, and in whose interest.

**The Student Movement demands transparency, the preservation of all evidence, and the establishment of the responsibility of all parties who acted contrary to the law.**

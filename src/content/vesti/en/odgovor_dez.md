To Informer,

**We hereby wish to inform you, clearly and unequivocally, that representatives of the Student List – Students Win will not take part in your television station's programmes.**

We consider this decision fully justified, given your years-long practice of violating every professional standard, the Law on Electronic Media, and the Code of Ethics of Serbian Journalists. **No representative of our list wishes to appear as a guest on a media outlet whose employees, over the past two years, have put targets on the backs of students, future MPs of the majority in the National Assembly, citizens, and their families, calling them "Ustaše" and "terrorists".**

It is telling that you are suddenly interested in what our representatives have to say about current social and political events, when for the past two years your coverage of some of the largest gatherings in our country's history, and of the list that ultimately won the people's absolute support, has been shameful, to say the least.

Given the recent decisions of the Regulatory Authority for Electronic Media and the bans it has imposed on broadcasting some of your shows, it remains entirely unclear to us which show you were planning to invite us onto.

**The Winners.**

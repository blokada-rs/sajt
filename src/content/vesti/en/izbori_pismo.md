Dear people,

Thank you for all the patience you have shown so far.

Victory is, thanks to you, closer than ever.

For two years we have been enduring, in the hope that this day will come.

We have waited patiently and with dignity.

We believe that every day and every minute spent waiting was worth it — we ask you to wait a bit longer.

Do not give up. The queues may be long, but out will is stronger.

Thank you for showing that the future of Serbia is not shaped by others, but that it is shaped by you.

We invite you to gather tomorrow, November 17th, at **8:00** in **Pionirski Park** to see off the Student List candidates together before they head to the **National Assembly**.

**After the gathering**, we ask citizens to disperse peacefully and follow the session from their homes, cafés, and other places.

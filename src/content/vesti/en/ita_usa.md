**Representatives of the Student List – Students Win and students will meet today with representatives of diplomatic delegations from the United States of America and Italy.**

The meetings are an opportunity for a direct exchange of views with international partners and for discussing issues that matter for Serbia's future.

This evening, our candidates **Milana Pisarić, Dejan Radivojević, Dejan Šoškić**, and **Igor Mikitišin**, together with student representatives, will meet **Alexander Titolo** and a delegation from the US Department of State. The talks will focus on the rule of law and energy, areas of particular importance for building stable institutions, economic security, and Serbia's long-term development.

In addition, **Vladan Jovanović, Stanislav Milošević**, and student representatives will meet a delegation from the Embassy of the Italian Republic. This meeting, too, will be an opportunity to present our positions and exchange views on Serbia's relations with its international partners.

Our election victory has given us the responsibility to represent citizens' interests in talks with representatives of the international community as well. We want to build relations based on mutual respect, openness, and cooperation as equals, while consistently insisting on the rule of law, democratic values, and accountable institutions.

Talking to international partners does not mean abandoning our own positions or leaving decisions to others. We are ready to talk, to listen to different opinions, and to build partnerships, but we will make decisions about Serbia's future independently and responsibly, guided solely by the interests of its citizens.

**In international relations, we want partnership, not subservience; cooperation, not obedience.**

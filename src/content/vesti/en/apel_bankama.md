From the Student List to All Banks Operating in the Republic of Serbia

To the Management and International Headquarters of Banking Institutions Operating in Serbia

Esteemed Representatives,

Following the parliamentary elections held in Serbia on Sunday, October 25, in which the Student List reports having secured a majority of parliamentary seats, the country is entering an important period of democratic transition.

In light of the anticipated transfer of governmental responsibilities over the coming weeks, we call upon all domestic and international banking institutions operating in Serbia, as well as their respective parent companies, to exercise heightened vigilance and oversight regarding cross-border financial transactions and potential capital outflows.

We particularly urge banking institutions to ensure that international transfers, especially those involving public funds, state-owned enterprises, and public institutions, are processed strictly in accordance with applicable laws, verified supporting documentation, and legitimate contractual obligations.

This appeal is not intended to restrict lawful commercial activity or interfere with the independence of banking institutions, but rather to reinforce financial integrity, accountability, and transparency during this transitional period.

The Student List remains fully committed to ensuring the timely and uninterrupted functioning of Serbia’s public institutions, maintaining financial stability, and fostering sustainable economic growth.

We reaffirm our commitment to transparent, constructive, and long-term cooperation with domestic and international financial institutions, in full respect of established legal and regulatory frameworks.

We trust that the banking community will uphold the highest standards of professional responsibility in safeguarding Serbia’s financial stability and the interests of its citizens and international partners.

Thank you for your attention and cooperation.

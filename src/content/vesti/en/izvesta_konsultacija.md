Representatives of the **Student List – Students Win** attended consultations in the National Assembly today. In addition to **Mario Reljanović and Aleksandar Čupić**, representing our list, the meeting was attended by Ana Brnabić and Milenko Jovanov on behalf of the United Serbia list, the Secretary General of the National Assembly, Srđan Smiljanić, and representatives of the minority lists.

On **setting up the commission to verify MPs' mandates**, the other lists were informed of the Student List's position that the process should be completed **as quickly and efficiently as possible**. The Assembly administration will be informed in good time of the final agreement on the commission's members.

Our representatives **requested that the session continue immediately after the mandates are verified.**

The representatives of the other lists were reminded that the Speaker of the National Assembly, the number of Deputy Speakers, and their names **will be voted on by a majority at the session**. Since our parliamentary group **discusses all issues democratically and takes decisions in the same way**, the public will be informed in good time about the possibility of leaving a certain number of Deputy Speaker posts to the other parliamentary groups.

**Representatives of all the lists agreed that voting should be public.**

The composition of Assembly committees will be proportional to the size of the parliamentary groups, and committee chairs **will be decided by a majority vote in each individual committee.**

**International delegations** will be determined on the basis of a proposal by the Speaker, who will first consult the chairs of the parliamentary groups, in accordance with Article 26(2) of the Rules of Procedure.

Regarding the seating arrangement in the chamber, **our parliamentary group requested to be seated to the right of the chair.**

Our representatives **also asked to be given the handover record for the duties of the Secretary General and the Speaker**, so that it is known what they left behind, what state the National Assembly is in, and how far their possible responsibility extends. The representatives of the other lists and the Secretary General of the National Assembly agreed to this.

The Secretary General asked us to submit on Monday the name of the Speaker, the total number of Deputy Speakers, the names of our candidates for Deputy Speaker, and the names of the candidates for the mandate verification commission.

Since **17 November marks International Students' Day**, our representatives also asked for **a delegation of students to be allowed to attend the constitutive session from the gallery of the assembly hall.**

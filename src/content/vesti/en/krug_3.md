**Три дана на бициклима кроз приградска насеља и мања места**

_Support must reach everywhere. Both places where the protests are most discussed and there where the voices of citizens are less often heard. This weekend, students and members of the public are setting off on a tour around Belgrade – to meet people, talk with them, and show that no place is too far, and no one is too far removed from the common struggle._

From Saturday to Monday, from New Belgrade via Pančevo, to Mladenovac and back, cyclists will travel through suburban neighbourhoods, villages, and smaller communities that are often overlooked [lit. ‘remain under the radar’]. Alongside the cycling tour, students, candidates, and other supporters of the student movement will be going door to door, talking to residents and sharing information about the forthcoming elections and planned activities.

## **What is the route?**

## ![](@assets/images/IMG_20261017_114106.webp)

**Saturday, 17 October**

**Narodnih heroja Street [lit. Peoples’ Heroes] – Surčin – Jakovo – Surčin – Dobanovci – Ugrinovci – Batajnica – Zemun – Padinska Skela – Borča – Pančevo**

The first stop is Pančevo, where an event is scheduled to begin at 19:00 in Kralja Petra Square. The cyclists will spend the night there, before continuing their journey the next day through South Banat and the area around Smederevo.

### **Sunday, 18 October**

**Pančevo – Starčevo – Omoljica – Skorenovac – Kovin – Smederevo – Mali Požarevac – Mladenovac**

On the second day, the tour will pass through towns and villages in the municipalities of Kovin and Smedervo, before continuing to Mladenovac.

### **Monday, 19 October**

**Mladenovac – Sopot – Barajevo – Sremčica – Umka – Ostružnica – Čukarica – Narodnih heroja**

On the third day, the cyclists will return to Belgrade, touring places between Mladenovac and the capital, completing the loop at the place where they started. 

## **Support is built through conversation**

Every part of Serbia deserves to be heard. Thatćs why the journey begins this weekend – to reach people, to talk face to face, and to make sure that nobody is left out of a community that is winning.

**Three days, dozens of locations, and one idea, to connect, listen to one another, and together open up the space for change.**

**Students are winning!**

Dear citizens,

The student movement grew out of the desire to give the state back to its citizens, to free institutions from political influence and strengthen them, and to establish the law as equally binding on everyone. Our goal is a state in which institutions serve the public interest, not the interests of individuals and political organisations. The Studentska lista – Studenti pobeđuju (Student List – Students Win) won your trust and support with a clear mandate to govern the country in line with the programme it presented, to fight for justice and to put an end to corruption.

That is why we consider it necessary to state clearly that we **will not tolerate any attempt by individuals or groups to cash in politically on the Student List's victory, using it as an opportunity to pursue personal and party interests**. The student movement is not a political party, nor anyone's extended arm, and it firmly denies the claims by certain political actors in Niš that there was long-standing cooperation with them. The Student List remains consistent in pursuing the goals it set itself and for which it won the citizens' trust.

The support that certain political actors voluntarily gave the Student List during the electoral process is not a political stake that entitles them to any favour in return, political horse-trading or claiming the results as their own. We will not allow attempts at bargaining, misrepresentation or the imposition of personnel choices that run counter to the basic principles of the student movement and, in some cases, to the autonomy of the university. Every such case will be reported to the competent authorities, and those responsible will be prosecuted in accordance with the law.

Along with historic support, the Student List has also been given a historic responsibility. Our obligation is to persevere, without compromise, in fulfilling the promises we made to citizens. That is why our message is this: no compromise with the electoral will of the citizens, and no compromise when it comes to respecting the law and procedures. **We will not take justice into our own hands, because justice, legal certainty and the rule of law are exactly what we have been fighting for since day one**. We promised a state with different rules, not different rulers. And we are starting to keep that promise.

To those for whom this statement is intended, we say:

**You know who you are. Soon the people will know too.**

Did you know that elections for the national councils of national minorities are being held on 8 November?

There are 24 such councils in Serbia. They act as minority self-governments that represent the interests of national minorities in education, culture, information in their mother tongue, and the official use of language and script.

The Vlachs are an ethnic community whose kin-state is Serbia, and whose rights, territory, and way of life are seriously threatened by extractivist projects. Instead of protecting them, the Council has consistently approved the very things that pose one of the greatest threats to their survival.

**But do the Vlachs even have anyone to vote for?**

**They don’t.**

Only one list is on offer, and it has no legitimacy whatsoever. The list “United Vlachs for a United Serbia” was formed before 25 October. It is made up of worn-out cadres of the former regime, many of them directly involved in corruption and scandals, who use the national minority council to evade criminal liability.

The struggle for freedom cannot be complete while the voice of minority communities is abused and their institutions are put at the service of political and economic interests. **Serbia will not be fully free until all its minorities are free as well.**

We call on citizens to **stay calm and rely on verified information** about the operations of Poštanska štedionica. Measures to preserve the bank's liquidity are already being applied.

People close to the outgoing regime have put pressure on Poštanska štedionica's liquidity by suddenly withdrawing large sums of money. **Citizens must not pay the price of that regime's election defeat.**

We will do everything in our power, once the Student List officially takes office, to **protect citizens' interests and help preserve the banking system.** We call on the National Bank of Serbia and all competent institutions to take all necessary measures to protect the bank's customers and to keep the public regularly informed about the situation at the bank.

We call on the media, analysts, experts and everyone who speaks publicly on this subject to **check information before sharing it.** Spreading unverified claims and causing panic can further harm citizens' interests.

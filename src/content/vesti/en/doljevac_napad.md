**Doljevac attackers walk free: are there two kinds of justice in Serbia?**

On the day of the parliamentary elections, **25 October**, a group of people attacked polling board members and observers from our list in **Doljevac**. More than ten people were injured or otherwise harmed. Our list's polling board members were threatened on election day, and the threats continued afterwards. The Basic Public Prosecutor's Office in Niš is conducting **several proceedings against the attackers** for violent behaviour committed by a group, endangering safety, and intimidating witnesses.

**The attackers are not unknown.** Among them are **Aleksandar Tošić** and **Zoran Ljubić**, **brother of the Mayor of Doljevac Municipality, Goran Ljubić**. They are part of a well-known crew that the people of Doljevac have long associated with pressure, intimidation, and vote buying, and that has served the Serbian Progressive Party for years. This violence is not an isolated incident involving a few individuals. It is how the SNS and its local power brokers try to control elections wherever they think no one is watching.

On Tuesday, **10 November**, the injured parties and witnesses identified the attackers at a line-up in the police station. **Even then, the intimidation did not stop.** While they were inside, a group of six or seven men close to the attackers gathered in front of the building. For their own safety, the injured parties and witnesses had to leave through a side entrance. Despite all this, **the prosecution did not request detention**. Those who beat and threatened us will remain at liberty while they defend themselves. They now walk the same streets as the people they attacked and the witnesses in these proceedings.

**The prosecutors handling these cases at the Basic Public Prosecutor's Office in Niš are Tatjana Milošević, Sandra Apostolov, and Vladimir Stanojević.**

**We do not accept the authorities' decision to act in this way.** The case files do not make clear why this case is being treated more leniently than the attack on members of the mobile team in **Niš on 25 October**, when state authorities responded swiftly and firmly. Nor is it clear why the prosecution has not joined these cases, when they involve the same people and the same place where the offences were committed. When the attackers come from the ranks of the ruling party and its local power brokers, the justice system suddenly becomes lenient. **That is a message that encourages violence.**

We therefore announce that we will file a complaint with the Supreme Public Prosecutor's Office about the work of the prosecutors handling these cases and of the Basic Public Prosecutor's Office in Niš, and ask for their work to be reviewed. **We call on the prosecution to publicly explain its decision not to propose detention for the identified attackers, and to reconsider it, in order to prevent the offences from being repeated and to stop pressure on the injured parties and witnesses, who still fear for their own safety and that of their families.**

**We will not back down.** Every threat and every blow only proves that we are right. Doljevac belongs neither to the Ljubić family nor to the SNS, but to its citizens. We will follow these proceedings to the end and keep the public informed at every step.

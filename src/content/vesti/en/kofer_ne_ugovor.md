We warn the outgoing government, which has lost all political legitimacy, not to use the last days of its term to **install party loyalists in public enterprises and institutions.**

We will not let you make it even harder to free public institutions from party influence. **Public institutions are not party spoils**, and jobs funded with citizens' money must not be a reward for political loyalty.

## Suspected new party hires

Reports of suddenly announced competitions for permanent jobs in schools, kindergartens, and other public institutions in Novi Sad, Inđija, Šid, and other cities raise **serious suspicion that the outgoing government is trying to secure jobs for its own people before the change of government.**

## Demand to suspend new hiring

We demand **an immediate suspension of all new hiring in public enterprises and institutions** until a thorough, independent, and transparent review of staffing, job classification, and the justification for new hires has been carried out in each of them.

After that, hiring must take place solely through **lawful, public, and transparent competitions, on equal terms for everyone**, based on expertise and competence, not party membership cards.

## Schools and kindergartens are not party strongholds

Education is one of the most important foundations of a society that wants to change and develop. **Schools and kindergartens must be places of knowledge, expertise, and equal opportunity**, not the last strongholds of party hiring.

Employees in public and municipal enterprises have been exposed to pressure and blackmail. **We're done with that.** In a time of accountability, the law and justice prevail.

## Accountability of heads of institutions

We warn heads of institutions that their work, too, will be under scrutiny, and that if it turns out they pandered to the demands of the fallen regime instead of serving the public interest, **they will face the sanctions provided for by law.**

**Public institutions belong to citizens, not to political parties.**

**Enough party-based hiring at citizens' expense!**

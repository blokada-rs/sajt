The National Assembly of the Republic of Serbia today elected Aleksandar Čupić, the candidate of the Student List – Students Win, as Speaker of parliament. **140 MPs** voted for him.

<br />

![](@assets/images/IMG_5069.webp)

<br />
According to the voting results, the following were elected Deputy Speakers of the National Assembly:

- **Adem Mavrić** – 137 votes
- **Marija Bjelobrk** – 136 votes
- **Sara Valentik** – 136 votes
- **Lidija Dimitrijević** – 136 votes
- **Tibor Lazar** – 136 votes
- **Jelena Petrović** – 136 votes

<br />
The other candidates received six votes each: Lidija Vukićević, Miloš Vučević, Arnaud Gouillon, Vladimir Đukanović, Marina Raguš, and Marija Stanković.


**Mirjana Radaković** was elected the new Secretary General of the National Assembly with 138 votes.

<br />

![](@assets/images/IMG_5085.webp)

<br />


The newly elected leadership comes from a range of professional and academic fields. Among them are university professors and teachers, medical specialists, political scientists, and artists.

<br />

![](@assets/images/IMG_5079.webp)

<br />

Aleksandar Čupić is an assistant professor at the Faculty of Transport and Traffic Engineering of the University of Belgrade, while Marija Bjelobrk and Lidija Dimitrijević are university professors and medical specialists. Jelena Petrović is a specialist in internal medicine and endocrinology. Adem Mavrić is a political scientist with a background in international relations, Sara Valentik holds a master's degree in political science, and Tibor Lazar holds a master's degree in painting and works as a teacher.

**The new leadership also reflects Serbia's national diversity.** The elected Deputy Speakers include Adem Mavrić, a Bosniak from Novi Pazar; Sara Valentik, a member of the Slovak national community from Bački Petrovac; and Tibor Lazar, a member of the Hungarian national community. Their election means that different national communities are represented in the leadership of the country's highest representative body.

The election of the new leadership opens a new chapter in the work of the National Assembly, in which the elected MPs will take on responsibility for the work of the highest representative and legislative body of the Republic of Serbia.

**After the arrest of the Mayor of Smederevo and the director of the Public Utility Company** in Smederevo over an illegal landfill in the village of Radinac, **garbage collection has stopped**. On 14 and 15 November, the people of Smederevo woke up to mountains of rubbish next to containers and bins.

**Smederevo students** emailed the Public Utility Company and the Deputy Mayor on 15 November, but received no reply to the email, and therefore no answers to the key questions. A day later, the Public Utility Company "Zelenilo i groblja" Smederevo issued a statement on the situation, saying it was doing everything in its power under the current circumstances, and it also put out additional garbage disposal bins. In the meantime, the City Emergency Management Headquarters held a session, and **a state of emergency was declared in Smederevo**.

**The people of Smederevo are worried.**

They tell us they have seen rats, both live and dead, at many busy locations in the city. Panic is spreading in the city, and in the villages too, through social media posts with photos of overflowing containers and rubbish piled around them. The grounds of the "Sveti Luka" hospital in Smederevo have also been affected, and horrific scenes of rubbish can be seen there as well. Schools have not been spared either. The same scenes can be seen right at the entrances to the schoolyards of primary and secondary schools in Smederevo.

Serbian Progressive Party MP Ana Brnabić posted a video in which she claims that the "new government" has brought Smederevo to this state. **We would remind everyone that the Serbian Progressive Party and its regime are still in power in Smederevo.** The illegal landfill, where fires have often broken out, has been a problem for the city for years. Meanwhile, media outlets that still do the old government's bidding are trying to blame the Student List for this problem in Smederevo.

**Who is really to blame for this situation?** Citizens have been raising the alarm about the landfill for years. The outgoing government did nothing about it, and fires break out a few times a year. Now the health of all the people of Smederevo is also at risk, after years of suffering environmental problems caused by the nearby steelworks and air pollution.

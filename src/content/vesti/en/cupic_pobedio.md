MPs today elected Aleksandar Čupić as Speaker of the National Assembly. **All 134 Student List MPs present voted for him, as did several MPs from the minority lists.**

MPs voted on the election of the Speaker of the National Assembly **by public roll call**. Each MP was called by name and stated aloud whom they were voting for.

## Final voting results:

Of the 239 MPs present:

- Aleksandar Čupić: 140
- Stojan Radenović: 97
- Abstentions: 2

<br />

## The responsibility of presiding over the National Assembly

Presiding over the National Assembly carries great responsibility. It is not only about chairing sessions, but also about **responsibility for the dignity of parliament, respect for the rules, and the equal standing of every MP.**

We believe that through his work, Aleksandar Čupić will help restore the reputation of this institution and of the office he holds. The National Assembly must be a place where every MP is respected, where different opinions are expressed freely, and where decisions are made through **serious, well-argued debate.**

## First among equals

Our Speaker will be **first among equals.** His role will not be to stand above MPs, but to ensure the conditions in which each of them can do their job on an equal footing.

We wish him every success in carrying out this responsible duty and a successful term.

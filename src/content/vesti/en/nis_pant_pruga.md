On 25 October, the citizens of Serbia made a clear decision: the era of impunity is over. The era of accountability begins. We are announcing the opening of investigations in the institutions and the prosecution of those responsible for the corruption we have been watching for years, in accordance with the law, without revenge and without exceptions.

One of the major examples of questionable dealings in our city is the high-speed railway through Pantelej.

The route plan for this railway was adopted in 2016, at a time when the City Assembly had been dissolved. The project, worth around 300 million euros, is called a "bypass", even though it merely moves the railway from one neighbourhood to another.

The draft provided for the demolition of around 20 houses and the closure of Borska Street. It was withdrawn only after street actions by local residents, a petition with more than 5,000 signatures and the submission of over 1,000 objections. The plans were changed even after permits had been issued and expropriation had been carried out. A safety study on freight trains passing through the neighbourhood has still not been presented to the public.

The works have still not been halted. On site, a deep trench is being dug, in which reinforcement for a concrete structure is being placed and tied. The contractor's workers and machinery carry on as if nothing had changed.

We expect all works in Pantelej to be halted immediately until all contracts, permits and decisions relating to this project have been examined.

We will not stay silent in the face of injustice and breaches of the law. **No one is alone!**

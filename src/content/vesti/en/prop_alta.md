**Alta Banka has lost its operating licence – preserving financial stability comes first!**

On 30 October 2026, the National Bank of Serbia revoked Alta Banka's operating licence because the bank was undercapitalised and illiquid. Alta Banka's problems did not arise overnight, and they require a thorough investigation.

In June 2026, Alta Banka reported a capital adequacy ratio of 17.92 per cent, more than double the basic statutory minimum of 8 per cent. Put simply, according to its own reports, the bank held twice as much capital relative to its business risks as the basic minimum required.

This raises a serious question: how did a bank showing such figures only four months ago end up losing its licence? Did the financial statements give a true picture of the bank's condition, were its loans collectable, and what did the National Bank of Serbia know and do?

There are indications that this situation results from the failure of the regulator and of the leadership of the National Bank of Serbia, headed by Governor Jorgovanka Tabaković, to react in time, as well as from the prosecution's failure to act and to freeze corruptly acquired assets.

Thanks to the efforts of several relevant actors, including some expert and honourable people at the NBS, more serious consequences for the citizens of Serbia were prevented. The measure that was issued prevents further irresponsible handling of the money of citizens and businesses, as well as further financial instability. In the coming days, legal steps need to be taken to secure the stability of the financial system, to place Alta Banka under control, and to prevent a similar scenario at other banks. Citizens' money up to 50,000 euros is insured and will be paid out.

## The situation is stable

**The situation is stable – there is no need for panic or for sudden, hasty reactions.**

The situation is serious, but at moments like this, hasty and sudden reactions are the greatest enemy of financial stability and security. According to assessments by the International Monetary Fund and by the National Bank of Serbia, the banking system is well capitalised, stable and liquid. In addition, the withdrawal of larger sums is regulated by the National Bank, so appropriate supervision will prevent further attempts at destabilisation, and money will be paid out in smaller tranches. We call on citizens to stay calm, to follow our statements and those of the National Bank and other relevant state institutions, and to make their decisions on the basis of verified information.

## Your money is insured up to 50,000 euros

**Your money is insured up to 50,000 euros, and the Deposit Insurance Agency's guarantee fund exceeds one billion euros!**

Under the Law on Deposit Insurance, money in current and savings accounts is insured by law up to a total of 50,000 euros per person at one bank, in accordance with the banks' internal regulations. This means that citizens whose funds do not exceed this amount are entitled to the payout of all their insured money. Funds above 50,000 euros can also be recovered through bankruptcy proceedings, but not necessarily by operation of law.

Within 7 working days of the court's decision to open bankruptcy proceedings, the Deposit Insurance Agency, which has been appointed as bankruptcy administrator, is obliged to enable the payout of legally protected funds to natural and legal persons, that is, to citizens and businesses. There is no cause for concern: the Agency's total funds exceed one billion euros and are enough to cover the need several times over. Official instructions on how and where payouts will be made will be available on the Agency's website www.aod.rs. There are reports that payouts will begin as early as 2 November.

## Next steps

As regards the stability of the financial system, we believe that Alta Banka is the just the tip of the iceberg of corruption, but not the only case. There are lawful ways to prevent further instability, and we urge that they be taken as soon as possible to preserve financial stability at this moment:

- \* Enhanced monitoring of the liquidity of all banks operating in Serbia.

- \* Readiness of the National Bank of Serbia to provide liquidity instruments (funds) to all solvent and soundly run banks, in order to preserve the stability of the healthy part of the banking system.

- \* Temporary measures at banks where it is established that there is an intention to bring them into an illiquid position.

- \* A rapid response by the competent authorities and the prosecution, to establish individual responsibility for abuses, corruption and lack of oversight.

- \* Timely informing of the public about the actions taken to preserve financial stability.

**As regards Alta Banka:**

- \* Alta Banka's problems require a detailed investigation of its business over a number of years. In June 2026, the bank reported to the NBS a capital adequacy ratio of 17.92%, well above the statutory minimum. It must be established how its financial condition deteriorated, whether the financial statements faithfully reflected the risks, and whether the NBS reacted in time.

- \* The assets of the Alta Group must be examined. We demand a review of all large loans to related parties, of guarantees issued, and of the possibilities for collecting receivables or recovering unlawfully transferred funds, in order to give depositors and creditors the greatest possible protection.

- \* It must be established who made decisions about the money of public enterprises. In particular, we demand an explanation of the decision to move the collection of Elektroprivreda Srbije's bills to Alta Banka from July 2025, and of the terms on which EPS and other public enterprises did business with the Alta Group.

- \* Responsibility must be individual and complete. We demand an independent analysis of the loan portfolio and the publication of a chronology of the NBS's supervisory measures, so that the responsibility of the owners, the management and the regulator can be separated. If criminal offences are established, those responsible must be prosecuted, with penalties that reach 10 to 12 years in prison for the gravest forms of certain abuses.

## Change of the EPS account for paying electricity bills

Because Alta Banka, through which electricity payments have been made until now, has ceased operating, EPS has changed its payment account number. The new account number is 200-75409-96, and it applies from the October bill onwards, as well as to the settlement of earlier debts. The payment reference number stays the same, while payment through the "EPS Uvid u račun" app is temporarily unavailable.

It is the responsibility of the institutions to protect citizens' money, and the responsibility of all of us not to react hastily or in the heat of the moment. Mass withdrawals from other banks can artificially create instability and illiquidity where there would otherwise be no problems. Citizens have the right to security, timely information and the protection of their funds. It is therefore important that the competent institutions act quickly and transparently, and that citizens stay calm, keep proof of their payments and follow official notices, so that they can exercise their rights as guaranteed by law!

## Financial security is not a political issue!

Through people close to it, the outgoing regime has shown that it is willing to endanger the entire system, out of anger at citizens over losing the election. We will take all necessary measures to preserve the banking system, and we call on the NBS and all other institutions to take an active part in this process. For us this is not a question of politics, but a question of the state and its citizens.

We stay calm, we make our decisions with care, and together we protect Serbia's financial stability and the well-being of our citizens!

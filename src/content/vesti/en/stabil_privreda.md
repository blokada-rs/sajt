We regard the situation at **IDEA Marketi d.o.o. Beograd**, whose change of ownership took place under suspicious circumstances, as a matter of great importance for **more than 7,000 employees, over 600 suppliers, consumers, and the overall stability of the retail market**. The company is systemically important: with total revenues of **RSD 93 billion (€800 million)**, it holds a share of over **15%** of the retail market. At a time of heightened uncertainty, the most important thing is to **prevent any disruption** of business and the consequences it would have for workers, domestic producers, suppliers, and citizens.

We are encouraged by the responsible, level-headed, and constructive approach of the vast majority of suppliers, who are owed **over €180 million** without any guarantees. According to the information available to us, most of them are ready to support the company's stabilisation, aware that its survival is, in the long run, in their own interest and in the interest of IDEA's employees and consumers.

At the same time, a thorough **investigation is needed into how the ultimate owner, Davor Macura, acquired ownership of the company**, the source of the funds used to finance the acquisition, and any links between companies that did business with **Alta banka** and the acquisition and its financing. **Establishing responsibility must not come at the expense of employees, suppliers, and citizens.**

Once formed, the new Government will **urgently, responsibly, and transparently present measures to stabilise IDEA's operations and protect everyone in the supply chain**. In the interest of employees, suppliers, and consumers, it is important that all business partners act responsibly, continue to cooperate, and leave room for dialogue and sustainable solutions, so that additional pressure does not threaten the stability of the system before the new Government sets out clear steps. **We will implement measures that will calm the market, protect suppliers and employees, and ensure that the burden of this crisis is not shifted onto those who live from their own work.**

**The stability of the economy, job security, and the protection of domestic suppliers will be among the priorities of the new Government of the Republic of Serbia and the Student Movement.**

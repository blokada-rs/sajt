The Higher Court in Pančevo has quashed the verdict against our colleague Andrej Tanko.

This decision is not an acquittal.

With this decision, **Andrej’s case goes back to the Basic Court, this time before a new judge**.

We remind you that these proceedings dragged on through months of uncertainty, hearings, pressure, and house arrest. All of it was over absurd charges, and over a verdict that found Andrej guilty of obstructing an official in the performance of their duties. Although the Higher Court’s decision is not an acquittal, it is an important step, and it confirms that the fight for truth and justice continues with even greater determination.

Justice must not be a privilege for the few. It must be equal for all.

**Together with Andrej, the fight goes on!**

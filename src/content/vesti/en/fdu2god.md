There are days that, once written down, never lose their place in the history books. **22 November 2024** is one such date. On that day, people who gathered peacefully in front of the Faculty of Dramatic Arts, were attacked.

## Two years since the attack outside FDU

Two years have passed since that day, bringing our country a sea of historic change. That is not what this is about. Instead, let us point to the case of five petty henchmen of the former ruling party who, acting in an organised and coordinated way, through their brutality became the backbone of the students' second demand and wrote 22 November 2024 into history.

## Five names and no accountability

Three of them, **Milija Koldžić, Ivan Stanišić, and Milena Aleksić**, entered into plea agreements. The criminal complaints against the remaining two attackers, **Dušan Kostić and Aleksandar Jokić**, were dismissed.

Such a shameful outcome could have been the epilogue of the story that began on 22 November 2024, had that story's protagonists been someone other than us.

## New days are coming

Today, **22 November 2026**, is the last day of the week in which the Assembly was constituted. Its Speaker and Deputy Speakers are candidates of the **Student List – Students Win**.

New days are coming for our country, for our institutions, and therefore for every citizen of this country.

**New days are coming for Milija Koldžić, Ivan Stanišić, Milena Aleksić, Dušan Kostić, and Aleksandar Jokić.**

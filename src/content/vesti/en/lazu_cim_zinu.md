The Student List is not yet in the Assembly, and those who lost the elections are still sitting in the Government and the ministries. This is a new form of manipulation by the former government, which, even in a caretaker capacity, still holds the institutions.

**Pensions are being paid into citizens' accounts, and the banks are open.**

We call on the police and the competent public prosecutor's office to establish urgently who drew up the fake document, allegedly issued by the ministry, that threatens people with the loss of their pensions and with prison sentences. Who printed and distributed it? Who ordered it, and who was the political instigator behind this operation?

</br>

![](@assets/images/2026-11-13%2011.14.41.webp)

</br>

**We demand an investigation into the criminal liability of everyone involved**, in particular for the possible criminal offences of forging a document and causing panic and disorder.

Our pensioners deserve respect and security, not to be targets of political manipulation and intimidation.

At the same time, we put a question to those responsible: **why did Banka Poštanska štedionica transfer to the Post Office only half of the funds needed to pay out pensions**, when the budget account of the Republic of Serbia holds around €2.5 billion, enough for roughly six months of pension payments?

Who made that decision, and on what grounds?

**Citizens deserve a clear answer, not artificially created uncertainty.**

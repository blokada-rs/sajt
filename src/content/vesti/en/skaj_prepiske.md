Following a report by the news outlet Radar, **information of public importance** emerged on 22 October, yet the competent prosecutor's offices have still not opened proceedings into its claims.

The article contains information that directly links former President Aleksandar Vučić to an organised criminal group led by Darko Šarić and Veljko Belivuk, who are currently held in the Central Prison in Belgrade.

After the arrest of Marko Kričak in July 2026, following a report by Veselin Milić, it is clear to us that the prosecution service intends to do its job while it holds its full mandate. We encourage prosecutors to continue acting in accordance with the law and not to feel under pressure, but they must also bear responsibility, both in this case and in other cases that implicate Veselin Milić's criminal liability.

It is extremely important that the competent prosecutors open an investigation and examine the allegations in the media, and that they establish whether the Sky messages could potentially be the subject of criminal proceedings against private citizen Aleksandar Vučić.

**In this regard, we call on the Supreme Public Prosecutor, Zagorka Dolovac**, to appoint a competent prosecutor to investigate these allegations, who would open pre-investigation proceedings to establish the criminal liability of private citizen Aleksandar Vučić.

**In this regard, we call on the Public Prosecutor for Organised Crime, Mladen Nenadić**, to open a criminal case against all individuals who took part in this undertaking and to gather the documentation needed to pursue it.

**The Sky messages are currently held by the Ministry of Internal Affairs**. They were obtained from EUROPOL, which forwarded a message from Eurojust informing the Ministry that, from 1 December 2026, the French authorities will no longer accept requests for mutual legal assistance seeking data from the Sky ECC application for use in court, whether submitted through Eurojust or through diplomatic channels, and that all requests received before 1 December will be executed.

**In this regard, we call on the Ministry of Internal Affairs** to urgently designate a department within the Criminal Police Directorate to investigate the messages and deliver the necessary documentation to the competent prosecutor's office, which will conduct the investigation to establish the criminal liability of former ruling officials responsibly and without pressure.

Although the levers of power are still in the hands of the Serbian Progressive Party, the handover of power to the Student List is inevitable. In this transition period, it is necessary to appeal to those currently holding office in state institutions not to give in to pressure from a single political party or individual, not to be afraid because they are doing their job, and not to become accomplices in destabilising the state and causing panic among citizens. They hold a full mandate, and before them stand the Constitution and the law, which they must respect.

**The Zone of Togetherness: At Slavija Square There is Space for You!**

We know that there are many people who want to be with us at Slavija Square on 22 October, but big crowds make it difficult for some to participate. We do not wish for anyone to stay at home because of that.

This is why we have secured a special space which we have called ‘The Zone of Togetherness’.

This space in front of Dimitrija Tucovića Square is intended for persons with disabilities, older individuals, pregnant people and those who wish to participate, but cannot or do not want to be in a big crowd.

For deaf persons, those hard of hearing and those who use sign language there will be interpreters translating during the event.

We want everyone who wishes to come to Slavija Square to have the chance to be here. To hear or by sign language follow the speeches. To be with their friends, family and fellow citizens and to feel that they are a part of the gathering.

Togetherness means that we think of one another, leaving no one behind.

See you on 22 October on Slavija Square!

Victory is Calling — Students Are Walking to Slavija Once Again!

_Do you remember those roads, the exhaustion soon forgotten,  the people who welcome us, and the feeling that, step by step, we had crossed something together that seemed impossible? It's time to set off again. Victory is calling!_

Students are setting off on their journey once again. Just like before, through towns and villages, across kilometers that bring people together and erase all distances. Once again, a succession of footsteps, reunions and hugs follows. Once again, the familiar feeling is reborn that no matter how long the journey, no one walks it alone.

This time, all roads lead to Belgrade, to Slavija Square. Students and citizens from across Serbia are coming to the final gathering, with the same wish and the same determination. Those who have not given up, who have persevered, and who believe that together we can bring about change, are coming.

**Victory is calling. From the north, from the east, and from the west. And at Slavija, we will meet again.**

See you at Slavija!

**Victory is calling! Answer the call!**

**22.10. at 18.00.**

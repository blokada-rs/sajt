The decision of a private citizen, the former President of the Republic, to pardon the driver charged with attempted aggravated murder for running over our colleague Sonja in January last year is unacceptable. 

This decision empties the institution of pardon of its meaning and sets yet another dangerous precedent in its use.

A pardon is an exceptional legal institution that must be used in rare and justified cases, not as an instrument of political revenge and personal score-settling. Conduct like this undermines trust in institutions and the very foundations of the legal order.

The task of the future government will be to ensure that the constitutional power to grant pardons is exercised transparently and responsibly, with clear criteria and accountability mechanisms that prevent its political abuse.

**May it never happen again.**

We have reason to believe that a certain interest group is trying to stage an incident in Belgrade tonight.

According to the information available to us, the alleged target of the attack will be the Informer newsroom, and they will try to shift the blame onto the students.

We have a clear plan: we will free the media in the National Assembly, through an institutional fight for media freedom and professional journalism.

We call on you not to respond to calls of this kind and to treat any information that circulates with a critical eye. Do not spread unverified claims, and do not let yourselves be drawn into a scenario that could endanger anyone's safety.

**We know how important it is to keep the peace and our dignity. They will not force on us the image of students as violent people.**

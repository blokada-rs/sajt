# Student List representatives to meet at the Embassy of Canada

At the invitation of the Embassy of Canada, representatives of the Student List – Students Win will meet **Ashley Durec**, Canada's Deputy Head of Mission and Political Counsellor, and **Vladan Miladinović**, who handles political and economic affairs and media relations at the Embassy.

The meeting will be attended by Student List – Students Win candidates **Aleksandar Baucal, Tamara Adamov, Ana Dimitrijević, and Vladan Jovanović, as well as students**.

The meeting will be an opportunity to get to know and talk with representatives of the Embassy of Canada, and to build relations and communication with international partners.

We will continue to keep the public regularly informed about the activities of our list's candidates and every meeting they hold. **Nothing happens behind closed doors.** Citizens will have insight into our work and the conversations we have.

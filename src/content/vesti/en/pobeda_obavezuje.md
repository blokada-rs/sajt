After a magnificent and unambiguous election victory, it is important that we stress a few things.

**The electoral process is still under way**, and it is up to us to secure every seat so that our future government is even more stable and strong.

The outgoing ruling regime's concession of defeat does not replace legal and institutional procedures. It is not for us to pass judgement on those who violated human rights and captured the institutions; that is the job of the institutions, which we will free once the government is formed.

Our victory does not mark an end, but the **beginning of a long process of rebuilding our country.**

**Anyone who has not wronged their neighbour has no reason to be afraid.** Those who have wronged the state and our society will be held accountable through the institutions once the government is formed and our candidates have taken their oath in the National Assembly.

**Anyone whose guilt is proven will answer before the law.** With this we will begin the process of rehabilitating our state and establishing functioning institutions that work for the people, not for personal and party interests.

We understand the euphoria, the impatience and the pent-up anger. We know you expect justice, and so we assure you that there will be justice, that the institutions will finally start doing their job, and that our future members of parliament will stand up for all of you.

The outgoing regime will do everything it can to show us in a bad light, from stirring up hatred and provocations to staging incidents. But, as before, we will not fall for their cheap tricks. We will continue to uphold the dignity and honesty that have marked our struggle.

**Our struggle is non-violent.** We have held to that until now, and it is how we secured victory. Now we must keep the maturity and dignity that our nationwide movement upholds.

**Thank you for the trust, patience and dignity you have shown.** We want to see your smiling faces, both in Pionirski Park and in every neighbourhood, because we know how much you, like us, have given for this victory!

**Representatives of the Student List visited Kosovo and Metohija**, where they went to the Gračanica and Visoki Dečani monasteries, as well as Prizren. During the visit, they spoke with Metropolitan Teodosije of Raška and Prizren and with the Abbot of Visoki Dečani Monastery, Archimandrite Sava Janjić, about the position of the citizens of the Republic of Serbia in Kosovo and Metohija, the challenges they face every day, and the responsibility we have towards them.

**The visit to Gračanica, Visoki Dečani, and Prizren** was an opportunity to remind ourselves once again of the importance of preserving our cultural, historical, and spiritual heritage, and also to turn our attention to those who live, work, and raise families in Kosovo and Metohija, and who, despite many hardships, strive to preserve their homes and their way of life.

Their perseverance deserves respect, but respect is not enough. **The citizens of the Republic of Serbia who live in Kosovo and Metohija need security, institutional support, and concrete solutions to the problems they face every day.** Their lives and their future must not depend on political interests, nor may their problems be reduced to a topic raised only when it is politically convenient.

**The conversations with Metropolitan Teodosije and Father Sava** confirmed the importance of **preserving our holy sites**, the Serbian Orthodox Church, and our national identity, as well as the need to approach the position of citizens in Kosovo and Metohija responsibly, with an understanding of the real circumstances in which they live.

To the citizens of the Republic of Serbia in Kosovo and Metohija, we say: **you are not alone, and you are not forgotten**. You have the right to a peaceful and dignified life, to safety, education, work, and a future in your own homes. **Your rights, safety, and dignity must be a lasting priority for the state and for society as a whole.**

For the Student List, Kosovo and Metohija **is neither a political slogan nor a way of scoring political points**. It is a matter of history, identity, responsibility, and, above all, people. We do not want a politics of grand promises with no deeds behind them, but a politics that starts from citizens' real needs, their rights, and the conditions in which they live.

We thank Metropolitan Teodosije and Father Sava for their hospitality, their open conversations, and their spiritual support.

**The Student List considers the preservation of the Serbian Orthodox Church, our holy sites, and our cultural and national identity, as well as the protection of the rights and safety of the citizens of the Republic of Serbia in Kosovo and Metohija, to be matters of the highest importance.**

We are aware that trust is not earned through statements and promises, **but through deeds**. That is why our visit is not the end of the conversation about Kosovo and Metohija, but a confirmation of our commitment to making sure that the voices of the citizens who live there are heard, that their problems are not neglected, and that their future is never the subject of political bargaining.

**The citizens of the Republic of Serbia in Kosovo and Metohija must know that they have not been left to fend for themselves. Their safety, their rights, and their dignified life remain our responsibility.**

On Friday, **13 November**, representatives of the Student List – Students Win, **Mario Reljanović and Aleksandar Čupić**, will take part in **a consultative meeting with representatives of the other electoral lists** that will make up the newly elected National Assembly of the Republic of Serbia. Four days before the constitutive session, scheduled for 17 November, they will discuss how that session will run.

## What is on the table

Our participation is limited to technical and organisational matters needed for the constitutive session to run efficiently. This means agreeing on the seating arrangement of the parliamentary groups, setting the agenda, and the question of who will chair the session, including establishing who the oldest MP is, since they will preside over it.

## What is not on the table

We are aware of the political context and of who the people we will be talking to are. That is why we are saying it in advance: consultations on technical matters will not become a space for political deals and negotiations behind closed doors. Our mandate comes from the trust of citizens and commits us to openness, accountability, and transparency. We will stick to the clearly defined scope of the talks, and the public will be informed in good time about everything agreed and about how the meeting went.

## A parliament where people can see what we do

The newly elected National Assembly must begin its work in line with the procedures, but also with a clear message that political decisions are made responsibly, in full view of the public. The trust we won in the elections means that citizens have the right to know what we do in their name.

**The students won!**

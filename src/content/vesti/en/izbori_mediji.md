## Press Conferences on Election Day

**Studentska lista – Studenti pobeđuju** will hold regular press conferences on October 25, with additional conferences if necessary, starting at 7:00 a.m.

Accreditation applications for press conferences on Election Day are now closed. Access to the venue will be granted exclusively to accredited media representatives upon verification of identity.

For media outlets unable to attend in person, a **direct audio and video feed via RTMP** will be available for further distribution and broadcasting across media programs and digital platforms.

**Technical details for accessing the live feed:**

- **Protocol:** RTMP / RTMPS
- **Server URL and Stream Key:** Available upon request
- **Video format:** 1920 × 1080, 25 fps
- **Audio format:** AAC, stereo
- **Feed availability:** From 7:00 a.m.

We look forward to your media coverage of the event.

**Studentska lista – Studenti pobeđuju**

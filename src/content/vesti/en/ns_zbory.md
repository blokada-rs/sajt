While we are still fighting for justice, certain individuals and informal groups are trying to set up parallel decision-making centres and pass off other people's efforts as their own political capital.

For two years we have been building something that cannot be bought: **trust, solidarity and unshakeable strength.** We have shown that an organised people and the students can push the limits of the impossible. But the fight is not over, and we will not allow it to become a playground for other people's political games, reckless moves and private interests.

**Our fight is a fight for working institutions, without violence, chaos, and vulturing.**

## Who is trying to hijack the student struggle?

While we are still fighting for justice, certain individuals and informal groups are trying to set up parallel decision-making centres and pass off other people's efforts as their own political capital.

So we take this opportunity to remind you:

**We work with the Mreža zborova (Network of Citizens' Assemblies)** ([@mrezazborova](https://www.instagram.com/mrezazborova/)), the only legitimate, transparent and united organisation of citizen assemblies.

**We do not work with the following groups:** Zborovi Novog Sada ([@zborovinovisad](https://www.instagram.com/zborovinovisad/)), Budni do kraja, Kraj and LibertaNS ([@BudniDoKraja](https://www.instagram.com/BudniDoKraja/)), 100 posto Sad ([@100postosad](https://www.instagram.com/100postosad/)), and any of their sub-organisations.

**We regard these groups as a direct attack on the Student Movement.** For months now, they have been acting on their own behind our backs, misusing the students' name and, under the false pretext that "the students have given their support", setting up their own parallel bodies in order to appropriate our struggle.

The crowning act of their obstruction was a reckless attempt to poach and redirect the Student List's election monitors for their own promotion, which directly endangered election monitoring on the ground.

With what authority, and for whom, do people like lawyer Srđan Kovačević, Dragan Đilas's personal lawyer, work? Who gives them the right to act behind our backs, change the direction of the struggle through non-transparent plans and exert informal influence?

## A call to all well-meaning people of Novi Sad

We know that most people in these organisations joined with a sincere desire for change. That is why we appeal to all of you: **do not lend legitimacy to people who use your energy for personal gain and self-promotion.**

Leave the structures that are working against unity. **Join the Mreža zborova.**

**Let us not allow individuals to become, in our name, exactly what we have been fighting against from day one.**

**Our strength lies in intelligence, organisation and honest intentions. The victory belongs to everyone, but least of all to those who try to abuse it.**

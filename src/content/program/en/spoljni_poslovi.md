We will conduct  foreign policy according to the Constitution, guided by principal, balanced, and pragmatic priorities. These will focus on representation in relation with other states, international organisations and institutions, and on protecting national interests of the Republic of Serbia, its citizens, diaspora and legal entities abroad.

We will conduct foreign policy in line with the Constitution, with **principled, balanced and pragmatic foreign policy priorities**, focused on representation in relations with other states, international organisations and institutions, and on protecting the state and national interests of the Republic of Serbia, its citizens, its diaspora and its legal entities abroad. In accordance with the Resolution on the Protection of the Sovereignty, Territorial Integrity and Constitutional Order of the Republic of Serbia of 26 December 2007, **we will preserve military neutrality as Serbia's strategic commitment.**

**Staffing policy** in the Ministry responsible and in the other state bodies that carry out foreign affairs and diplomatic and consular representation will undergo a thorough reform that will **end the years-long practice of partocracy, nepotism, politicisation and appointments made without professional qualifications, professional experience or clearly defined criteria.** We will break with the practice of political actors promoting themselves and establish **systematic, long-term and institutional representation of state and national interests in international relations.**

With a new security and reputational assessment, **discretionary grants of citizenship and the passports issued will be reviewed as soon as possible**, especially where there is reasonable suspicion of corruption.

**We will preserve the diaspora's cultural ties with Serbia and its sense of shared identity**, and improve cooperation with the scientific and professional community of Serbs abroad. We will pay particular attention to **creating the conditions for the return of young professionals** who gained their education and professional experience abroad, and of other qualified people, with the aim of employing them professionally in the Republic of Serbia. Following successful practice in other countries, we will consider **setting up development funds to encourage diaspora investment**, especially in the less developed parts of Serbia.

## Kosovo and Metohija

**We will fight to keep Kosovo and Metohija within Serbia, in accordance with the Constitution of the Republic of Serbia and Resolution 1244.** We will consistently oppose the unilaterally declared independence of the so-called Republic of Kosovo and **will not allow it to join international organisations.** We will prosecute all responsible representatives of the regime for all potential criminal offences related to the conclusion and implementation of unconstitutional agreements to Serbia's detriment: **all agreements must be subject to a review of their constitutionality**, and implementing any unconstitutional provision of any agreement concluded is unacceptable.

**We will make a special effort to improve the position of Serbs in Kosovo and Metohija**, both north and south of the Ibar, both in the six Serb-majority municipalities and in places with a significant Serb population outside these municipalities.

We will work to link such places administratively and include them in Serb-majority local self-government units, and for those units to achieve **the greatest possible autonomy from the Provisional Institutions of Self-Government in Pristina.** We will make particular efforts to **create the conditions for the survival of the Gorani national minority**, and to re-establish a local self-government unit in the areas where members of the Gorani community are the majority.

**We will preserve our educational, health, social and cultural institutions in Kosovo and Metohija**, with particular emphasis on the University of Pristina, temporarily based in Kosovska Mitrovica, and we will protect our cultural and religious heritage, as well as the property of the Serbian Orthodox Church.

## Serbs in the region

**We will foster good-neighbourly relations and develop regional cooperation, in accordance with all the principles of international law**, with all neighbouring states, while deepening bilateral and multilateral relations with the states of the region, as well as transport links and economic and energy cooperation with the member states of the Danube Commission, the Craiova Group and the Visegrád Group, CEFTA, and other relevant regional cooperation organisations and initiatives.

**We will preserve the constitutional position and established competences of Republika Srpska**, including its right of veto within the joint institutions, as well as its movable and immovable property. In cooperation with the international community, we will review the practice to date of applying the so-called Bonn powers of the High Representative for Bosnia and Herzegovina, and insist on **a legal and legitimate selection of the High Representative for Bosnia and Herzegovina**, in line with the signed agreements and with the necessary consent of the United Nations Security Council. We will alert the international community and use all necessary diplomatic and legal mechanisms in every case where the mandate set out in Annex 10 of the Dayton Agreement is exceeded, especially where this results in encroachment on and violation of the rights of the entities guaranteed by the Constitution of Bosnia and Herzegovina. In accordance with the Agreement on Special Parallel Relations between the Republic of Serbia and Republika Srpska, **we will strengthen cooperation and ties** in the areas of economic exchange and development, industrialisation, the improvement of transport and energy infrastructure, agriculture, services and tourism, public security, integrated development, inter-university, educational and cultural cooperation, and other areas of public policy.

**We will work to improve the status of the Serbian people in Montenegro** and to secure the rights set out in the constitutional guarantees that protect identity and prohibit assimilation, as well as **to resolve the issue of dual citizenship**, by concluding a bilateral agreement or finding another appropriate legal solution that would allow the issue of dual citizenship to be resolved to mutual benefit.

**We will insist on the rights of the Serbian people in Croatia and on the preservation of their national, cultural and linguistic identity** in line with the highest European and international standards. We will pay particular attention to the position of returnees, to the selective application of the law in certain court proceedings and to the conditions for dignified commemoration at grave sites, as well as to respect for United Nations Security Council Resolution 1120, the Letter of the Government of the Republic of Croatia (S/1997/27), the Erdut Agreement and the 2004 Agreement between the Republic of Croatia and Serbia and Montenegro on the Protection of the Rights of the Croatian Minority in Serbia and Montenegro and the Serbian and Montenegrin Minority in the Republic of Croatia.

## Serbia and Europe

**The European Union is the Republic of Serbia's most important economic partner**, with foreign trade that reached almost 60% in 2025. The 2008 Stabilisation and Association Agreement enabled duty-free exports of most domestic goods and services, opening up one of the world's largest and most developed markets to the domestic economy. This agreement was also a key step towards a visa-free regime for citizens of the Republic of Serbia under the Schengen white list, giving them a freedom of movement they had never had before, as well as access to IPA pre-accession funds and other significant development funds. These made possible important investments in the renewal and construction of capital infrastructure and in improving key social systems, to the benefit of all citizens of the Republic of Serbia.

**We will deepen the strategic partnerships already concluded with European Union member states** (Greece, Italy, Hungary, France), and seek similar forms of intensive bilateral cooperation with other European states.

**We will seek to obtain IBAR status through significant progress in the judiciary and fundamental rights**, as well as on justice, freedom and security, the areas covered by Chapters 23 and 24. We will deepen cooperation with the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development and the European Investment Bank, in order to **secure grants and other financial support for development projects of importance to citizens.**

## Serbia and the world

**We will preserve our traditional partnership with the Russian Federation** on coordination in the United Nations Security Council, on protecting Serbia's territorial integrity and on resolving open issues of common interest. We will hold to the consistent position that imposing sanctions on any state, entity or individual reflects a political stance, and does not show a conviction that sanctions help to resolve political and security challenges, especially in view of our own historical experience and the direct effects of such measures on the local population. **The Republic of Serbia will maintain its principled position of not imposing restrictive measures on the Russian Federation.** At the same time, we will hold to the principled position of international law on respecting the territorial integrity of all sovereign countries in the world, including the integrity and inviolability of the territory of Ukraine, while consistently working for **an end to the war, the peaceful resolution of disputes and the best possible relations with both friendly peoples.**

**We will develop relations with the United States of America based on common interest and greater trust.** We will continue the strategic dialogue on telecommunications, energy, transport, artificial intelligence, gas diversification and LNG, and defence cooperation, and **we will further increase trade and the arrival of high-tech investment that brings long-term development.** We will involve the Serbian diaspora in the US through institutional cooperation mechanisms, for investment and the exchange of knowledge, and we will build relations with America for the long term, independently of short-term political changes.

**We will develop our comprehensive strategic partnership with the People's Republic of China**, our most important trading partner in Asia, and expand cooperation from the economic, infrastructure and industrial spheres to high-tech investment. In all future projects, we will insist on **respect for domestic regulations, environmental standards, workers' rights and occupational safety.**

**We will open new markets for domestic exports and renew our diplomatic presence in Latin America, India and Africa**, building on our traditional friendships from the era of the Non-Aligned Movement. We will expand scholarships for students from African countries, and with Latin America we will also strengthen cooperation in the fight against organised crime.

**Our people know well what it means to be the victim of genocide and ethnic cleansing, and that is why we will oppose every form of oppression of the weaker by the stronger.** Accordingly, **we will not be complicit in the systematic attempt to destroy the Palestinian people that Israel is carrying out in the territory of Gaza**, and we will step up the sending of humanitarian aid to Palestine to save human lives and reduce the number of victims.

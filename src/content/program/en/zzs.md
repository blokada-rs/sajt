We protect water, air, soil, nature and biodiversity as the highest public interest. Our environmental protection system is built on personal and institutional accountability, the alignment of regulations with the highest standards, and independent impact assessments for mining, infrastructure, and industrial projects. We will establish systemic monitoring and ensure public access to pollution data, enabling automated responses from inspections and competent authorities to any threshold breaches, alongside consistent enforcement of penalties as well as the involvement of science, education, and citizens.

Citizens have the right to correct, transparent, and diverse information, as well as to media that work in the public interest. We must  protect the freedom of reporting from political and economic pressure, and make public-service broadcasters  independent and accountable to the public. We need conditions in which different opinions can be heard without pressure and intimidation, protection of journalists, and transparent financing of the media.

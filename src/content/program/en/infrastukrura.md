Infrastructure should connect people and places and to answer to the public’s actual needs. We will develop roads, railways, public transportation, telecommunication networks and urban spaces based upon long-term plans, public consultation, and transparent financing. Urban planning must protect quality of life and public spaces. Every public project must be subject to independent oversight and accountability for the quality of the work carried out.

Agricultural policy must ensure timely and predictable incentives, particularly for small and medium-sized farms, as well as better opportunities for young people who wish to remain in rural areas.

There is a need for more accessible financing, more reliable market placement for domestic products, and investments in storage, processing, and irrigation. The goal is to enable farmers to plan their production, make a living from their labor, and develop rural communities, while preserving fertile soil and natural resources.

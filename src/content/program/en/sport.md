The quality of elite sport depends on the breadth of its base, and the base here is the children who train and the families who pay for it. Today the state gives money to clubs and federations, so it is up to them who gets to train and on what terms. **We must see sport as preventive medicine, not as a race for results.** To change this perspective, we need to reverse the flow of money. **The money will follow the child and the athlete.**

## Sports voucher

**Every child in primary and secondary school will receive a sports voucher; the entitlement is automatic, with no means test and no application.** The amount is the same for every child and is set by the ministry each year. The voucher can be used to pay for a sports programme, and the ministry will make and check the payments. Any voucher that is not used expires. Vouchers can be used only at accredited clubs listed in the public register, for a maximum of two programmes a year, and children who are not in the school system can claim theirs through the Centres for Social Work. **Every community, including rural ones, must have at least one accredited club.**

## Membership fees and transfer releases

**Making a child's transfer release or registration papers conditional on a development fee will be banned by law.** Such a condition will be legally null and void, and anyone who imposes it will face misdemeanour and criminal liability. **A club that receives public funds will charge membership fees only up to a limit set by the ministry**, and charging more than the limit, or charging "off the books", will mean a permanent loss of public money and criminal liability for the club's management.

## PE from the first grade

We will propose to the National Education Council that **physical education in grades one to four be taught by a PE teacher**, and that structured physical activity be introduced in nurseries.

## Scholarships for elite athletes

**Elite sport will be funded by a Serbian Olympic Fund, which will receive its money from lottery and sweepstakes revenue, not from the budget.** This earmarked money will be protected, and the Government will not be able to reallocate it, even in extraordinary circumstances. **The fund's scholarships will be paid directly into athletes' accounts, at three levels.**
## Expertise and athlete safety

We are introducing **a national licensing system for all sports staff, with four levels and a public register**, as well as **an independent National Centre for Safety and Integrity in Sport**, with a platform called "Siguran sport" (Safe Sport) for reporting violence and abuse.

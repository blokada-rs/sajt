The quality of elite sports depends on the strength of its grassroots base itself, in this case, that base consists of the children who train and the families who fund it. Today, the state allocates funds to clubs and federations, leaving it up to them to determine who trains and under what conditions. We must view sports as preventive medicine, rather than a mere race for results. To shift this perspective, we need to direct the flow of money. Funding must follow the child and the athlete.

Young people must have a genuine opportunity to take part in decisions that affect their lives. They need to have accessible education, support in finding their first job, more affordable housing, and more opportunities in culture, sport, and youth organising. We want young people in Serbia to be able to plan independent lives, develop their ideas, and participate in society without feeling that leaving the country is their only chance for a future.

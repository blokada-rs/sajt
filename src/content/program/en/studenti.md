What students get from the state today in terms of funding depends on the city and the bank. Scholarships have no fixed payment date, public transport discounts differ from city to city, and the student canteen in Novi Sad carries meals over to the following month, while canteens elsewhere do not. The accountability of teaching staff towards students depends on the "goodwill" of individual higher education institutions and their management, with no legal, contractual or statutory obligation to give students adequate protection, while students hold between a fifth and a quarter of the seats on decision-making bodies.

## Scholarships, transport and canteens

**Scholarships and loans will get a fixed payment date that is respected every month, with payment into an account at any bank.** We are considering reinstating partial loan repayment: a student with an average grade between 8 and 8.5 would repay half of their loan and everyone else the full amount, and in both cases repayment would begin once they are employed (see the Higher Education and Science section). **The student discount on urban and suburban public transport will be the same throughout the country.** On the route between the place of study and the place of residence, the discount on rail tickets will increase, following the example of Romania, where it is 90%, or Croatia, where travel is free. For students whose permanent residence is outside Serbia, we will plan in line with the budget. **Canteens will have the same subsidised and full-cost prices throughout the country, the option to buy individual meals instead of packs of ten, and meals carried over to the following month as in Novi Sad.** This will come with a national nutrition standard and greater capacity. We will also ensure that **accommodation in student halls is available during the summer months at the same prices as during the academic year.**

## The assembly, the parliament and student protection

**An assembly of all students enrolled in the current year will become a legally recognised form of organisation, with the right to bring initiatives before the institution's bodies.** We are making individual candidacies possible for the Student Parliament, and student organisations will receive mandatory, evenly distributed funding. **At every faculty, students will be able to reach out to someone when their rights have been violated, and will be able to count on that process being effective.** Complaints will be accepted anonymously, and the activity report will go to the Faculty Council and be published. **We will introduce disciplinary liability of teaching staff towards students** and change the rules on ethical responsibility so that sanctions match the offences committed.

## Paid traineeships and youth offices

**A Law on Work Placements will require a contract between the employer and the trainee, pay that the employer must provide, mentoring, a limited duration and training goals.** This prevents employers from exploiting young people as free labour in the name of a traineeship – the cost to the employer becomes young people's first paid job.

**Every municipality will have a Youth Office and a Youth Council, with youth budgeting and a standard for youth spaces**, and youth workers will be brought into schools and Centres for Social Work. **The National Assembly will get a dedicated Committee on Youth.**

Economic policy must encourage domestic manufacturing, innovations, and stable working positions, rather than reward the privileged and politically connected. We will spend the public funding responsibly, with clear priorities, transparent contracts and oversight of results. We want predictable terms for businesses, a fairer tax system and an economy where people can live with dignity from their work.

Gender equality is not only a human rights issue, but also a prerequisite for social justice, freedom from all forms of exploitation, and the foundation of a society built on solidarity. That is why we shall tackle obstetric violence, increase maternity and pregnancy-leave payments, and require employers to eliminate unjustified pay gaps. We will impose the harshest penalties for discrimination on grounds of gender, age, or any other characteristic.

Gender equality is not only a human rights issue, but a precondition for social justice and liberation from all forms of exploitation, and one of the foundations of social justice and of a democratic, solidarity-based society. **We will reinstate and improve the Law on Gender Equality, with mandatory mechanisms for eliminating inequality, oversight of its implementation, and sanctions for institutional discrimination.** Every law and public policy will undergo careful analysis, and to that end we will strengthen the work of the Coordination Body for Gender Equality.

## Equal pay for equal work

We will strengthen the Labour Inspectorate, introduce **uniform and transparent pay grades in collective agreements**, and require employers to eliminate unjustified differences. **We will impose the strictest penalties for gender, age and all other forms of discrimination.**

## Pregnancy and motherhood without penalty

We will strengthen public healthcare and accessible gynaecological and reproductive care, and prevent obstetric violence. **A person chosen by the woman giving birth will be able to attend the birth free of charge.** We will guarantee the return to the same job.

Compensation will be calculated on the basis of the highest-earning month in the previous 12 months (for pregnancy leave) or 18 months (for maternity leave), with a top-up for lower earnings up to a solidarity threshold. **We will extend the maximum number of days of childcare leave from the standard 365 to 547 days for the first and second child.** The condition for this extension will be that the father takes at least 182 days of childcare leave. For fathers who own small and medium-sized businesses or work in agriculture, the form of leave will be adapted.

## Inheritance that actually happens

**We will tighten oversight of renunciations of inheritance and penalise coercion and blackmail.** We will expand free legal aid in inheritance proceedings and **give women in rural areas equal access to land, credit and public funds.**

## Menstrual health

Menstrual products will be taxed at **the reduced VAT rate of 10%, like food and medicines.** Dispensers with free pads will be installed in the toilets of public institutions. **We will also introduce menstrual leave**, with particular attention to people with PMOS and similar disorders. VAT on products for babies and children up to the age of five will be reduced.

## Public care instead of unpaid work at home

We will develop and restore **free, publicly funded services**: public transport, nurseries, crèches, after-school care, care homes for older people, day centres and home care for people with severe disabilities, distributed according to need.

We will work for education and media policies aimed at overcoming gender stereotypes and building a culture in which **care, caregiving and housework are a shared social and family responsibility, not solely an obligation of women.**

## A life free from violence

To protect women and prevent violence, we will actively work on **establishing a legal definition of femicide**, and to that end we will monitor the social and cultural background of crimes in which women are the victims. We will work on **establishing safe houses with stable public funding** and on introducing free legal and psychosocial support to protect victims of violence.

We will strengthen the police and the anti-trafficking unit, **toughen penalties for pimping, and prevent forced and child marriages.** We will ban conversion therapy.

For years, we have been told that long queues and waiting are a fact of life, and that the same is true in many more developed countries. The time has come to show that it can and must be otherwise. We will build a healthcare system in which doctors know their patients. A system in which the patient trusts their doctor. A system in which emergency medical services reach every village just as quickly, and no one has to choose between buying medicine and buying food. We will build a system in which no one feels abandoned. We will restore professionalism to healthcare workers, security to patients, and dignity to all citizens of our country.

The exploration and exploitation of mineral resources must be based on expert assessments, publicly available data, and accountability to people and nature. Before decisions are made on mining and geological projects, it is essential to independently assess their impact on water, soil, health, and the lives of local communities. Citizens must be informed in a timely manner and actively involved in the decision-making process, alongside consistent oversight and strict enforcement of penalties for all regulatory violations.

People who build Serbia through their work deserve to be able to live with dignity on what they earn. From the first wage to old age, everyone deserves security they can rely on. We will fight for a state that stands by its citizens even when they lose their jobs, become ill, or need support. The wealth of our country must belong to the people who create it!

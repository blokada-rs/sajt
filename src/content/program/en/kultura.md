With some of the lowest incomes in the country and spending that does not exceed 0.7% of the public budget, culture is in a dire position in our country. The risk is borne by individuals, whose work has for decades been invisible, underpaid or unpaid, which leaves this field almost completely deregulated. Entire specialised professions are dying out in heritage protection institutions because of the hiring ban, while what remains is being taken over by investor-led urban planning. **We reject the model of the creative industries and the commercialisation and festivalisation of culture. Artists will be recognised as having the status of employees, and culture will become a social good funded from the public budget.** We are taking heritage and institutions out of the hands of investors and party-appointed acting directors and giving them back to the people and the profession.

## The culture budget and the status of artists

**At least 1% of the national budget will be allocated to culture**, and we will also increase the share of local budgets, while abolishing populist budget items and spending on activities that do not belong to culture.

**Independent artists and professionals will be put on an equal footing with employees.** The base for their insurance contributions will be the national average wage, the state will pay taxes and contributions for registered activity, and debts arising from administrative errors will be written off urgently. The special collective agreement, as well as the base salaries and coefficients of employees in cultural institutions, will be revised, and trade union organising in culture will receive support. **Salaries and the running of institutions will no longer be funded from projects; funding calls will be intended solely for programmes.**

## Culture on television, in schools and in small towns

**The law will set the goal that science and education, culture and arts, documentary and children's programming make up at least 30% of the total annual broadcast output on national frequencies**, as a condition for new and renewed licences, under the rules in the Media section. Through amendments to the Law on Advertising, we will ensure free advertising for cultural content. We will propose a mandatory cooperation protocol to schools, cultural institutions and arts associations. **Cultural institutions will be required to organise free programmes for children and young people.** We will extend the network of local cultural centres and community cultural centres to small towns and villages, with a dedicated budget fund for renovating existing buildings and organising free programmes.

## Running cultural institutions

**Directors of institutions will be selected through public and professional procedures**, and institutions will be run through plenums and professional bodies, on a rotating basis. We will review all appointments of acting directors made without a competition or on the basis of party loyalty, as well as all hiring carried out outside competitions, under the rules and deadlines set out in the Economy and Finance section. **All student and youth cultural centres will be returned to the universities.** Students will be given full autonomy in running the centres in Belgrade, Novi Sad, Niš and Kragujevac.

## Protecting heritage

**We will annul the Government's decisions revoking the cultural monument status of the General Staff building, and restore that status to the General Staff building as well as to the entire Belgrade Fair complex.** The special law on the General Staff building will go through the same procedure as all special laws, with a proposal to repeal it within twelve months, and the contract with the investor will be reviewed, with an assessment of the arbitration risk. We will make the documentation on EXPO 2027 available to the public and subject its projects to the same review procedure in which all of the previous regime's contracts are re-examined (the Economy and Finance and Infrastructure sections), without a separate "cultural" review. **Heritage protection regulations will take precedence over planning documents, and every infrastructure project will have to undergo a heritage impact assessment.**

**We will halt construction of the garage next to the National Library of Serbia building and build the repository it needs**, and subject construction at Kalemegdan and Ušće to an expert review.

## Contemporary production, spaces and artificial intelligence

**Arts programmes will receive multi-year funding, transparent funding calls and tax incentives.** Public institutions will be required to share their spaces with other actors, and abandoned public spaces will be listed and made available to artists in line with a rulebook. **We will protect authors' work from commercial use in artificial intelligence**: we will propose a ban on training models on works without the author's consent, fair remuneration and mandatory labelling of machine-generated content, and we will prepare guidelines for international alignment.

**We will increase the funds set aside for international cooperation in the Ministry of Culture's budget**, and establish a system of ongoing research into culture, participation and programme evaluation.

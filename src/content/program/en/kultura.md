With some of the lowest earnings in the country, the cultural sector finds itself in an unenviable position. Because the risk is borne by the individual—whose work has for decades remained invisible, underpaid, or entirely unpaid—this field has become profoundly unregulated. Hiring freezes are causing entire specialized professions to vanish from heritage preservation institutions. We are ending investor control and politically appointed acting officials in heritage and these institutions, and returning them to the people and the profession.

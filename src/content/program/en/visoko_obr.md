Universities must have academic autonomy, stable funding, and conditions in which knowledge is valued independently of political influences. Students should have more affordable access to higher education and support throughout their studies, while researchers should have the conditions to carry out high-quality work and develop their careers in Serbia. By investing in science, research infrastructure, and industry collaborations, we are creating the conditions for knowledge to be a driver of development and for young experts to build their futures here.

Today, the rule of law in Serbia exists mostly on paper. The judiciary is under political influence, the police operate as an extended hand of the government and they oversee themselves, and elections take place without the public’s trust. These issues are interconnected, and thus we are solving them as a whole: through an independent judiciary, an accountable police in the service of the citizens, and fair electoral conditions.

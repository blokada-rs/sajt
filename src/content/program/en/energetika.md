The energy system must ensure a secure and affordable energy supply for households and the economy, alongside responsible management of public enterprises. Investments in energy efficiency and renewable sources should reduce costs, import dependency, and pollution. Decisions on energy must be transparent and long-term, with clear assessments of costs and impacts on citizens and the environment.

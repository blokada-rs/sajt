Tourism vouchers will be distributed through a transparent public points-based system according to household income and size. Higher-value vouchers will go to people with the lowest incomes and to people with disabilities. Applications will be accepted at the post offices, and some vouchers will be reserved for the off-season. Employer incentives will be tied to formally registered workers and properly paid wages. Work placements for pupils and students must include a mentor, a learning plan, and payment. Short-term contracts must not be used to replace permanent staff.

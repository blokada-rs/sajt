The defense system has to be professional, well-equipped, and ready to respond to foreign defense challenges. The members of the army deserve dignified working conditions, adequate training and equipment, as well as transparent rules for career progression. Defence funds must be spent responsibly and transparently, and the defense institutions must work in the interest of the country and its citizens and not in the interests of any political party.

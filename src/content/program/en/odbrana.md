The mass departures of officers, non-commissioned officers and professional soldiers, especially in 2017, together with the deaths in military units and the defence industry that today's system is still dealing with, point to one thing: **a clear and urgent need for change.** This tragic state of affairs in the armed forces is the result of poor financial management, or rather a lack of funds, outdated equipment and a shortage of qualified personnel. Instead, we stand for **a complete turnaround, that is, gradual modernisation, professionalisation and new technology**; in short, we will work to strengthen **quality, not quantity.**

**We will increase soldiers' basic pay.** There will be allowances for duty shifts, night work and dangerous tasks, and field allowances will be paid at the full daily rate. **A state housing loan with a subsidised interest rate of 1–2% will be made available to all military personnel**, which, together with additional measures, will help military personnel solve their housing needs.

We are introducing **six months of voluntary military service for around 3,000 volunteers**, who would receive at least 20,000 dinars a month, with the time counted towards their pensionable service, and with the aim of at least 25–30% of them going on to become professionals. **The active reserve would gain 3,000 reservists on permanent contracts**, serving up to 45 days a year with pay and a field per diem of around 5,000 dinars, a "thirteenth salary" or a 5–10% bonus, and advantages when applying for state jobs. **We will increase defence spending**, to be used for capital projects, war reserves and new capabilities.

**We are building an integrated, multi-layered air defence dome over the whole territory**, linked into the network-centric "SOVA 24" system in three layers by range: short range – 20–30 kilometres; medium range – 30–100/170 kilometres; and long range – 170–300 kilometres.

**We will modernise the Air Force according to a phased and financially measured plan**, with standing alert duty for rapid response. Logistics will be digitalised and based on domestic production, with **investment in the "Čačak" Technical Overhaul Institute.**

**We will modernise the defence industry's factories and turn them into smart factories**, establish **a National Centre for Military Electronics** in cooperation with technical faculties, and launch a defence software programme. The Military Technical Institute and the Military Academy will focus on developing complex combat systems in line with the needs of the Armed Forces.

**Deaths in the defence system**, such as those of the guardsmen at "Topčider" in 2004, the Mi-17 crash in 2015 and the sergeant during the "Munjeviti udar" exercise in 2021, **will be reopened and fully investigated**, and **the staff exodus of 2017 will be investigated**, in order to re-examine individual disciplinary and criminal responsibility.

A new commission will be set up – **an independent, inter-ministerial Government commission on irregularities, abuses and corruption in the production, procurement and export of weapons**, as part of the general audit of the previous regime's contracts (see the Economy and Finance section). Its work will be transparent, while of course protecting classified data, and **Yugoimport SDPR will return to its core business of arms trading.**

**Civilian and democratic control of the defence system will be applied consistently**, through the Government, the National Assembly, the Protector of Citizens and the Commissioner for Information, and **competent and uncompromised people will be appointed to leadership positions.**

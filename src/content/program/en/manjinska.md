All citizens should have the same rights and equal opportunities to participate in societal life, regardless of their national identity, language, or culture. Differences must not be a reason for exclusion, and the rights of minority communities must not depend on the goodwill of those in power. We want a Serbia in which identity and culture of every community are respected, and where no one has to choose between their distinct identity and participating in society as an equal.

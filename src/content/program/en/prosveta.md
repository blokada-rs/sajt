Every child deserves a high-quality education and the support they need to develop their abilities, regardless of where they live and the family’s financial circumstances. This requires better conditions in schools, support for teachers, up-to-date curricula, and a system that identifies learning difficulties early. Education should foster knowledge, critical thinking, and independence, and decisions. Decisions about education should be made on the basis of expertise and in the pupils’ best interests.

  # **The Serbia We Deserve**

  In the weeks following the November 1, 2024 tragedy, students blocked university faculties and, through plenums held across Serbia, adopted a set of demands that remain a cornerstone of the student movement today. Over time, it became increasingly clear that the issues raised by students’ demands were inseparable from the country’s future. Therefore, in early May 2025, the student movement demanded snap parliamentary elections, marking a new phase in which students, together with citizens, began to define the kind of Serbia we want and deserve.

  Today, our task is to turn that energy and unity into a clear vision for the future and concrete steps toward its realization. The Student List carries that vision forward as the vehicle for that journey.
